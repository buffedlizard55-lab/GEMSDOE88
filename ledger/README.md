# Ledger

`submissions.csv` reconciles **seventeen** built candidates: three `MASS` variants, one score-blind `PRUNE3`, four `CONCENTRATION` variants (the original 1 km / 5 km family plus `CONCENTRATION15pct`), the three-slot physics-ranked `PRUNE` sweep (`p1px`/`p3px`/`p4px`), and **two** `SNAP` families — three `R0`-based arms (`w2E1`/`w2S2`/`w2E2`) and three `R1`-based arms (`g2f35`/`d2f35`/`d1f35`). Every exact `(parent, axis, value)` pair and every primary/zeros SHA-256 is unique; the axis duplication is recorded in IR-88-25.

- **Parents:** `MASS`, `CONCENTRATION`, the `R0` `SNAP` family and `PRUNE` are edits of `R0 = h33-h33-2-b2`. The second `SNAP` family is an edit of `R1 = h33-h33-2-b2-PRUNEp3px-20261023-6f7ae705`, because a non-`PRUNE` axis on `R0` inherits `2,171` dots at `2 < d_cat <= 3 px` and breaks Brief §4.4. `parent` on every row names the raster that was actually edited.
- **Gate status:** only `PRUNEp3px`, `PRUNEp4px` and the three `R1` `SNAP` arms satisfy the literal `>= 3 px` collar. `MASS`, `CONCENTRATION` and the `R0` `SNAP` arms retain `579–2,171` parent-collar dots and are `HOLD`.
- **`SNAP` is retired (IR-88-27):** both families lose blind off-catalogue USGS `SGMC` DTI against their own parent (`R0`: `-0.0040 … -0.0080`; `R1`: `-0.0010 … -0.0041`), and the loss tracks displacement. Nothing in either family is a slot candidate. Spend slot 1 on `PRUNEp3px`.
- **Predictions:** `predicted_change` is always labelled `MODEL:` and is never a score. `MASS` predictions come from PR #17's audit (`registry/mass_ranking_audit.json`); `CONCENTRATION` from `registry/concentration_projection.json`; `PRUNE` from the exact marginal-DTI sweep; `SNAP` from its mechanism cards (which keep both the a-priori and the post-measurement prediction where they differ).
- **Re-runs are family-scoped:** `scripts/build_prune_variants.py`, `scripts/build_snap_variants.py` (the `R0` family) and `scripts/build_snap_r1_variants.py` (the `R1` family) each replace only their own rows, so rebuilding one family never deletes another.
- **Rasters:** competition rasters stay out of Git (data-use terms unchecked). Every family keeps hashes, gate receipts and rebuild commands in `releases/*-receipt.json` or the enriched `releases/<id>.json`; the older `MASS` rasters were tracked before that policy and are left in place.
- **Mechanism cards:** `mechanism_cards.json` holds the audited `MASS`, `CONCENTRATION`, `PRUNE` and `SNAP` cards; the `PRUNE3` card sits beside its release receipt in `releases/`.

Before any future build, re-check `origin/main` **and** this ledger for the exact `(parent, axis, value)` pair and every primary/zeros SHA-256. Never treat a `MODEL` or `PROXY` value as a receipt score.
