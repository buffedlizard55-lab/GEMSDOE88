`submissions.csv` reconciles **forty-one** built candidates across all seven single-axis experiments: three `MASS` variants (`m10minus`, `m10plus`, `m20minus`), ten `PRUNE` variants (`3`, `p1px`, `p3px`, `p4px`, plus the R0 collar sweep `p3.2px`, `p3.7px`, `p4.3px`, plus the refill-mode arms `p3norefill`, `p3space35`, `p4space35`), four `CONCENTRATION` variants (`b10px-m10`, `b10px-m20`, `b50px-m20`, `15pct-1km-grad`), twelve `SNAP` variants (on `R0`: three unspaced `w2E1`, `w2S2`, `w2E2` `HOLD` controls and three spacing-locked `d_nn >= 2.8284 px` `ridge2px`, `pot2px`, `scarp2px` variants; on `R1`: capped-gradient `g2f35`, `d2f35`, `d1f35`, plus the gate-clean scarp transplant `scarp2px` (`c003d7d7`), `scarp1px` (`1944aaf5`), `ridge2px` (`f4af84a3`) — the only family with all three arms above parent on the blind SGMC control and 100% of dots `>3 px`, IR-88-50), nine `RELIEF` variants (on `R1`: `up2t10`, `up2t15`, `dn2t15` plus the single-source brackets `rng2bas12`, `rng2pmt12`, `bas2rng12`; on `R0`, built concurrently and reconciled per IR-88-38: `basin12`, `pied12`, `range12`), three `SPACING` variants on `R1` (`poisson28`, `lattice4px`, `strike`; IR-88-48), and three Phase 2 `TRACKB` variants on `R1` (`speckle10minus`, `traceext10`, `tracespine10`, paired with `evidence/trackb_traces_geologist.geojson`; IR-88-49). Every exact `(parent, axis, value)` pair and every primary/zeros SHA-256 is unique. All rows are `BUILT`; no receipt field is filled and no slot is recorded as used.

- **Gate status:** `PRUNEp3px` (`6f7ae705`) and `PRUNEp4px` (`be9fd3d5`) satisfy the literal `> 3 px` collar across all `37,654` dots. New R0 collar arms `p3.2px`, `p3.7px`, and `p4.3px` also have minimum catalogue distances `3.6056`, `4.0000`, and `4.4721 px`; all six primary/fallback rasters pass `registry/prune_sweep_validation.json`. The spacing-locked `SNAP` (`ridge2px`, `pot2px`, `scarp2px`) and `CONCENTRATION` rows hold `100%` of moved dots strictly at `d_cat > 3.0 px` while inheriting R0's `2,171` `2–3 px` collar dots unchanged (`Same count, same exclusions`). `recommendation` on each row records its slot role and collar disposition.
- **PRUNE refill-mode arms (parent `R0`; PR #34):** `p3norefill` (`N=35,483`, collar purge with no refill, diagnostic only), `p3space35` and `p4space35` (count-fixed, refills spacing-locked at `>=3.5 px` from a widened `H19-5 ∪ H19-4 ∪ H16-1` pool). The two instruments disagree on them (field negative, blind SGMC `0.101042 / 0.102454` vs R0 `0.096060`); see their IR-88-44 and `evidence/refill_spacing_comparison.json`.
- **Predictions:** `predicted_change` is always labelled `MODEL:` and is never a score. `MASS` predictions come from `registry/mass_ranking_audit.json`; `CONCENTRATION` predictions from `registry/concentration_projection.json` and `ledger/concentration_build_receipt.json`; prior `PRUNE`/`SNAP` predictions from their axis reports; new sweep predictions from `registry/prune_sweep_preregistration.json` and receipts. No live score is inferred.
- **`RELIEF` family (parent `R1 = PRUNEp3px`, not `R0`; IR-88-33):** donors are the dots whose removal costs the least *exact* expected DTI under the label-free corroboration field `w` inside the source terciles; receivers are the unused ridge-support cells inside the target tercile with the largest exact marginal DTI gain, every one at `d_cat > 3 px`. All three arms are predicted **negative** (`MODEL -0.002 / -0.004 / -0.007`) because the parent already saturates the well-evidenced range front (IR-88-34); they are diagnostics, not slot candidates, and their hide-and-recover holdout is structurally `0.000000` (IR-88-35).
- **`RELIEF` R0 family (parent `R0`, `scripts/build_relief_r0_variants.py`; IR-88-38):** same tercile definition and the same exact-marginal ranking as the R1 family, but one dose (`12%`) in all three directions with proportional donor thinning, pre-registered in `registry/relief_r0_preregistration.json` before any proxy was run. All three arms lose on the blind `SGMC` control (`0.08664 / 0.08752 / 0.09314` vs `R0 0.09606`) and also with uniformly random donors, because only `90` un-emitted H19-5 pixels lie `>= 2.8 px` from an R0 dot: every relocation trades a spaced dot (`0.11–0.16` TP) for a densified one (`0.04–0.07` TP). Final `MODEL` `-0.010 / -0.008 / -0.003`; all `HOLD`. Report: `evidence/relief_r0_axis_report.json`; validator output: `registry/relief_r0_variant_validation.json`.
- **Rasters:** new competition rasters stay out of Git (`.gitignore` excludes `releases/*.tif` and `releases/*.zip`; data-use terms unchecked). `PRUNE`, `CONCENTRATION`, `SNAP`, and `RELIEF` keep hashes, gate receipts, and rebuild scripts in `releases/*.json`; the older `MASS` rasters were tracked before that policy and are left in place.
- **`SPACING` family (parent `R1 = PRUNEp3px`, not `R0`; IR-88-48):** one shared 1,914-dot greedy-decluster donor set (`5.08%`, survivors restored to min NN `2.8284 px`) with three receiver geometries on un-emitted H19-5 at `d_cat > 3 px` — farthest-point (`poisson28`, `MODEL +0.003`), 4 px lattice (`lattice4px`, `MODEL -0.004`), along-strike (`strike`, `MODEL +0.000`) — ranked by geometry only, no field or label. Blind SGMC ordering matches preregistration (`0.096820 / 0.095292 / 0.095396` vs `R1 0.096083`, all deltas `< 0.001`); all three `HOLD`. Report: `evidence/spacing_axis_report.json`; validator output: `registry/spacing_variant_validation.json`.
- **`MASS` `r10*` family (parent `R0`, `scripts/build_mass_variants.py`; IR-88-44):** the first mass arms ranked by the parent's own exact marginal expected-DTI under the label-free field `w` (dose 10% = 3,765 dots), preregistered in `registry/mass_r0_preregistration.json`. Both instruments flipped the sign of the two pure-mass arms, so predictions were revised by a pre-declared rule to `-0.008 / +0.003 / -0.001`; both the preregistered and revised values sit in every card and row. A random-donor control (`evidence/mass_r0_ranking_control.json`) splits each blind-control delta into a pure dose effect (`-0.006230` at `-10%`, `+0.001102` at `+10%`) and a `w`-ranking effect (`+0.001756` / `-0.001831`): **R0 sits at its mass optimum**, so `MASS` is retired as a slot-spending axis. Only `98.2%` of additions fell within `3 px` of an existing dot — every refill/relocation axis draws from the same saturated H19-5 pool. All three arms inherit R0's 1,611 sub-3 px dots, so under the corrected non-PRUNE gate (PR #36 / IR-88-47) all three are **HOLD**.
- **SNAP scarp transplant family (parent `R1`; `scripts/build_snap_r1_scarp_variants.py`; IR-88-50):** the R0 blind-control winner's exact field (`edge_scarp_pot` = 0.42 b19 slope + 0.23 b12 Hessian ridge + 0.20 b18 iso-grav HG + 0.15 b3 TMI HG percentile ranks; provided bands only, zero external LiDAR) and exact spacing-locked allocator on the gate-clean parent, preregistered in `registry/preregistration_snap_r1_scarp.json` before any proxy ran. Blind SGMC `PROXY`: `scarp1px 0.097567`, `scarp2px 0.097453`, `ridge2px 0.096548` vs `R1 0.096083`; the w-field scores the off-support arms negative (`-0.035 / -0.077` MODEL), recorded only as a diagnostic (blind AUC `0.47`, IR-88-38). Hardened-validator output: `registry/snap_r1_scarp_validation.json` (6/6 PASS); axis report: `evidence/snap_r1_scarp_axis_report.json`.
- **Mechanism cards:** `mechanism_cards.json` holds structural-geologist cards for all eight experiment families: `MASS` (both rankings), `PRUNE` (incl. refill-mode and spacing-locked), `CONCENTRATION`, `SNAP`, `RELIEF` (both parents), `SPACING`, `TRACKB`, and the `MASS` `r10*` family above (43 cards).
