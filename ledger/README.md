# Ledger

`submissions.csv` reconciles **38** built candidate records: three `MASS`, ten `PRUNE`, four `CONCENTRATION`, nine `SNAP`, nine `RELIEF`, and three `SPACING` variants. Every exact `(parent, axis, value)` pair and every primary/zeros SHA-256 is unique. All rows are `BUILT`; no portal receipt fields are filled and no slot is recorded as used.

- **Parent and evidence:** `R0 = h33-h33-2-b2` has `37,654` dots and was rebuilt byte-exactly against both recorded TIFF hashes. Its historical `0.2778` is `CLAIM`, not a receipt. `predicted_change` always means `MODEL`, never a score; report-only holdout/SGMC values are `PROXY`.
- **Gate status:** most non-`PRUNE` R0 descendants inherit the parent’s `2,171` dots in the 2–3 px catalogue collar and remain `HOLD`. The PRUNE and SPACING receipts identify their own literal collar disposition; do not infer an unrecorded gate pass.
- **`PRUNE` refill-spacing family:** `p3norefill`, `p3space35`, and `p4space35` are separate refill-mode treatments, not repeated radius aliases. `p3space35` is conditional only; the two report-only instruments disagree. See `evidence/prune_spaced_report.json` and `evidence/refill_spacing_comparison.json`.
- **`SPACING` family:** `lattice`, `poisson`, and `alongstrike` use parent `R0`, fixed count, legal `>=3 px` catalogue support, and the same `5,376` relocations. Their only arm-to-arm treatment is ordering of replacement cells. Their shared collar repair and weaker `sqrt(5)` guard make all three `HOLD`, not weekly-slot recommendations. See `registry/spacing_preregistration.json`, `evidence/spacing_axis_report.json`, and `evidence/spacing_first_attempt_failure.json`.
- **Rasters:** new competition TIFF/ZIP artifacts remain local and gitignored; tracked JSON receipts preserve hashes and format evidence. The older tracked files are historical and are not silently redistributed.
- **Mechanism cards:** `ledger/mechanism_cards.json` contains 37 cards for the currently documented candidates; the historical `h33-h33-2-b2-prune3-20261023-01d57649` ledger record has no card. Cards capture hypotheses and disconfirmers, not scores.

Before any future build or upload, check exact `(parent, axis, value)` and primary/zeros SHA-256 duplication. Before a human upload, revalidate the actual disk bytes and obtain the human-controlled quota/deadline/receipt information. Never treat a `MODEL` or `PROXY` value as a receipt score.
