## Scientific hypotheses vs confounders

- **Potential-field co-edge (tested MGC-88, Experiment 1):** a structural juxtaposition *may* generate magnetic (`rtp`, Band 2) and gravity (`iso_grav_anom`, Band 13) gradients in the same place; a buried trace might be missing from a surface catalogue. But an **unfaulted lithologic contact** does too. Misregistration caused by depth/dip and *independent* survey processing may alter alignment. The fixed blocked test (`gems57-pooled-hide-v2`, `n=22,893`) supported a small known-catalogue proxy increment (`HOLDOUT-DTI 0.0389 [0.0264, 0.0532]` vs raw control `0.0267 [0.0173, 0.0372]`, paired $\Delta = +0.0122\ [0.0056, 0.0197]$), **not** competition eligibility. No direct vent prediction. [Official source for accessible magnetic/gravity layers](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/#provided-features).
- **Conductivity–gravity lateral offset (tested CGO-88, Experiment 2):** testing a 200 m cross-strike offset between gravity (`iso_grav_anom`, Band 13; `iso_grav_anom_hg`, Band 18) and airborne EM surface conductivity (`cond_surf`, Band 17) conditioned on `depth_to_base_surf` (Band 15) scored **HOLDOUT-DTI `0.0227 [0.0154, 0.0309]`** (pure) and **`0.0230 [0.0162, 0.0308]`** (joint with MGC-88) on the identical 8 blocked folds (`gems57-pooled-hide-v2`, `n=22,893`), significantly below MGC-88 (paired $\Delta = -0.0162\ [-0.0281, -0.0073]$ pure; $-0.0158\ [-0.0286, -0.0061]$ joint). Why? On-disk audit (`registry/cgo88_holdout_and_band19_audit.json`) shows `cond_surf` has single-feature symmetric AUC `0.5038` on withheld fault segments and reflects broad playa/evaporite basin salinity rather than discrete fault traces, causing spatial cross-quadrant overfitting.
- **Zero-catalogue multi-physics curvilinear Hessian crests + spin-2 strike consensus + exact-shell submodular DTI allocation (tested STR-88, Experiment 3 of 3):** combining 18 baseline-invariant channels across `det_elev_slope`, `det_elev`, `iso_grav_anom_slope`, `iso_grav_anom_hg`, `iso_grav_anom_vg`, `tmi_hg`, `tmi_vg`, `rtp`, `tc`, `depth_to_base_surf` (local 2.5 km high-pass bench relief), `geod_dilaterate`, and `geod_shearrate` (excluding `cond_surf` and all raw regional DC levels) with prevalence-calibrated LOQO PU HGB and exact 6-shell Bernoulli-max `dinkelbach_allocate` (`min_spacing_px = 2.85`, `budget = 37,654`) scored **HOLDOUT-DTI `0.0997 [0.0878, 0.1110]`** on the identical 8 blocked folds (`n = 22,893`), beating MGC-88 (`0.0389`) on 8/8 folds by paired $\Delta = +0.0608\ [+0.0445, +0.0757]$ (**2.56× lift**) and beating greedy 2-px spacing (`0.0765 [0.0638, 0.0883]`) by $+0.0232$. Notably, 400 m spin-2 strike persistence (`spin2_mp_persist_400m`, symmetric AUC `0.5844`) integrates to zero around circular volcanic plugs/domes while reinforcing linear multi-physics fault corridors.
- **Acquisition artifact risk:** Area 2 line spacing 400 m corresponds to 4 cells on a 100 m raster. A nearly periodic signal of that spacing should trigger a flight-path/grid orientation control, not a geothermal interpretation. This is an *inference* from documented USGS acquisition parameters, not a verified fault detector.
- **Thermal evidence is contextual, not truth:** 2 m probes, well chemistry and paleo geothermal deposits in [GDR 1391](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391) can reveal fluid flow independent of the fault catalogue, but hot springs can follow permeable lithologies or groundwater conduits unrelated to a mapped surface rupture. Treat these as candidate corroboration for later tests, not hidden labels.
- **Proxy failure:** known-catalogue holdout tests interpolation/recovery of a distribution already mapped. New expert labels can be under alluvium or in a different survey block. A positive HOLDOUT-DTI contrast does *not* estimate the private test score. A second truly independent off-catalogue target would help, but a public fault compilation overlapping the provided labels can create circular evaluation; keep provenance and non-overlap checks explicit.
- **Metric economics:** for a non-redundant binary dot whose own truth-kernel credit k equals its incremental truth coverage, α+β=1 yields `Δdenominator=α` and `ΔDTI>0 iff k>α·DTI`. When incremental coverage differs from its own credit (redundant dots), use the exact denominator and max-cover implementation rather than this shortcut. No rule based only on dot count or a family score proves a portal result.

## Deep dive: faults vs geothermal vents — what the competition actually scores

> **Competition truth = faults.** The [problem page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) and [About page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/968/) describe geothermal systems as fault-controlled fluid pathways; the labelled truth is *geological fault traces indicative of geothermal resources*, not the vent or spring itself. A thermal spring can follow permeable lithology or a buried aquifer with no mappable shallow rupture; a blind geothermal system by definition has no surface vent. Detecting a vent directly does not satisfy the fault-discovery metric.

### Three outside-the-box but testable research directions (and what blocked testing showed)

1. **Cross-property offset (`CGO-88`, tested & falsified on holdout):** Testing a 200 m cross-strike offset between gravity (`iso_grav_anom`, Band 13) and subsurface conductivity (`cond_surf`, Band 17) yielded `HOLDOUT-DTI 0.0227 [0.0154, 0.0309]`, failing to beat `MGC-88` (`0.0389 [0.0264, 0.0532]`) because `cond_surf` is dominated by broad basin/playa brines (`AUC_sym = 0.5038`).
2. **Magnetic residual & tilt-curvature asymmetry (`MRR`, unrun):** Band 6 `tc` achieves `0.5608` symmetric AUC on withheld faults (second-highest of all 19 bands); pairing across-strike `tmi_vg` polarity asymmetry with `tc` targets buried fault/volcanic steps using only provided bands.
3. **Strain-locked curvature pairs (`TSC`, unrun):** Band 19 `det_elev_slope` achieves the highest single-band symmetric AUC (`0.5642`); requiring opposite-sign profile curvature transverse to GPS/InSAR strain (`geod_dilaterate`, `geod_shearrate`, `geod_2ndinv`) isolates tectonic flexure from Lake Lahontan shorelines.
