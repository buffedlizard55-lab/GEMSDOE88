## Scientific hypotheses vs confounders

- **Potential-field co-edge (tested MGC-88):** a structural juxtaposition *may* generate magnetic and gravity gradients in the same place; a buried trace might be missing from a surface catalogue. But an **unfaulted lithologic contact** does too. Misregistration caused by depth/dip and *independent* survey processing may alter alignment. The fixed blocked test supported a small known-catalogue proxy increment, **not** competition eligibility. No direct vent prediction. [Official source for accessible magnetic/gravity layers](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/#provided-features).
- **Acquisition artifact risk:** Area 2 line spacing 400 m corresponds to 4 cells on a 100 m raster. A nearly periodic signal of that spacing should trigger a flight-path/grid orientation control, not a geothermal interpretation. This is an *inference* from documented USGS acquisition parameters, not a verified fault detector.
- **Thermal evidence is contextual, not truth:** 2 m probes, well chemistry and paleo geothermal deposits in [GDR 1391](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391) can reveal fluid flow independent of the fault catalogue, but hot springs can follow permeable lithologies or groundwater conduits unrelated to a mapped surface rupture. Treat these as candidate corroboration for later tests, not hidden labels.
- **Proxy failure:** known-catalogue holdout tests interpolation/recovery of a distribution already mapped. New expert labels can be under alluvium or in a different survey block. A positive HOLDOUT-DTI contrast does *not* estimate the private test score. A second truly independent off-catalogue target would help, but a public fault compilation overlapping the provided labels can create circular evaluation; keep provenance and non-overlap checks explicit.
- **Metric economics:** for a non-redundant binary dot whose own truth-kernel credit k equals its incremental truth coverage, α+β=1 yields `Δdenominator=α` and `ΔDTI>0 iff k>α·DTI`. When incremental coverage differs from its own credit (redundant dots), use the exact denominator and max-cover implementation rather than this shortcut. No rule based only on dot count or a family score proves a portal result.
