2 · persistent thermal corridors
Potential: medium-high · cost: high		 Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 surface temperature, QA-masked multi-year seasonal medians and robust local anomaly; retain only strike-coherent persistent line segments. Repeat-season thermal expression may reveal shallow geothermal discharge or hydrothermal alteration above blind faults that do not produce reliable topographic scarps. 3 · groundwater head compartments
Potential: medium · cost: very high		 USGS Water Data for the Nation groundwater-level time series, well locations/depth and aquifer metadata; remove seasonal/pumping trends, estimate repeatable head offsets and cross-well hydraulic-lag coherence. A blind fault can act as a conduit or barrier, producing persistent hydraulic-head steps or time-lag boundaries not represented by a surface trace. Pumping, irrigation, seasonal recharge, different screened intervals, aquifer contacts and measurement-datum changes. The repository includes point well/spring inventory cues, not groundwater-level time series or a fault-compartment statistic. USGS groundwater records and services are public. The legacy NWIS interfaces are being migrated to Water Data for the Nation. Exact in-footprint well density, record length, units and data-use terms were not audited; do not treat the source catalog as proof of viable coverage.
4 · calibrated U–K disequilibrium
Potential: medium-low · cost: medium		 Official GeoDAWN raw K, U and Th grids plus scaling/flight metadata; compare regionally detrended U/Th and K/Th discordance on orientation-consistent 300 m ridges. Hydrothermal fluids can redistribute K and U relative to relatively immobile Th along structures beyond mapped faults. Unfaulted granitoid/intrusive contacts, soil cover, radon-background residuals and survey leveling seams. KTH-88 used an antisymmetric K/Th step from uncalibrated u8 indices; calibrated U/Th discordance is untried and distinct, but remains sensor/method-adjacent. USGS GeoDAWN is an official CC0 data release (DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ). Not yet viable: raw product, scale/offset, exact footprint and authorization were not authenticated; mirrored u8 layers are not adequate.
