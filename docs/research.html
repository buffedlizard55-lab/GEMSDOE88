Historical leaderboard-receipt calibration (LBCAL)

The data: 106 of the 111 user-reported LB scores have their exact raster on GitHub. DTI is linear in the truth indicator, so E[TPi] = Σ q·ci exactly.

The model: a coordinate-free density over 25 physical bands, q = exp(β·φ), fitted by least squares to the receipts. On 8-fold repo-blocked CV it scores RMSE 0.0306 and ρ 0.941.

What it found:

  • Truth is about 22× denser 1 px off the H19-5 ridge than on it.
  • The ≤200 m catalogue collar is empty: tilt-ring, 100k dots all within 2 px of the catalogue, scored 0.0005.
  • The off-ridge submodular family (gems70, gate_ortho, tiltworm) is under-predicted by 0.09–0.12, so a localiser is still missing.

This material is preserved for audit and is not a submission recommendation. The current canonical decision is the negative MRR-BPR-88 run card; see the current repository review and untested-hypothesis shortlist.

  • MODEL-PROJECTION: LBCAL 0.356, H33 0.264, PROVCREST 0.105.

Forensic answer

Why H33’s user-reported 0.2778 is scientifically plausible

No portal receipt was available, so 0.2778 is a CLAIM, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. The exact pixels still reveal the likely mechanism. H33 is a strict 37,654-dot subset of D2.8’s 44,090 dots. It deletes exactly 6,436 predictions within 200 m of mapped faults and leaves the distant support unchanged. With false-positive penalty α=0.2, this flank pruning can improve precision while preserving off-catalogue piedmont and basin coverage.

H33 places 68.4% of its support more than 1 km and 41.8% more than 2.5 km from the catalogue. That geometry avoids over-investing in already-mapped halos. Its discrete sparse support also avoids the large false-positive mass paid by continuous nonzero surfaces.

What cannot be inferred

H33’s mechanism does not make its reported score transferable to another raster. Organizer labels remain hidden. MRR-BPR-88’s HOLDOUT-DTI cannot be compared numerically as if it were a portal score, and its negative paired result provides no support for beating H33.

DTI = TPw / (TPw + 0.2 FPw + 0.8 FNw)

All scores on this page are explicitly HOLDOUT-DTI. ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED: none.
