H33 forensic interpretation

Why the reported 0.2778 is plausible, not verified

Repository-recorded pixel-lineage evidence is consistent with H33 being a thinned descendant of the H19-5/D2.8 family: later rasters remove many predictions close to the mapped catalogue while retaining broader support. Under a distance-weighted Tversky metric, removing redundant nearby predictions can reduce false-positive penalty while preserving coverage farther from mapped faults. The 6,436 removed D2.8 pixels being within two pixels of the catalogue is consistent with that mechanism; it does not prove that pruning caused the reported score.

H33's 0.2778 is user-reported, not organizer-confirmed. The repository records a public leaderboard snapshot dated 2026-10-24 with a top public value of 0.4413; that dated snapshot was not freshly fetched in this review and is not H33's receipt. HOLDOUT-DTI measures hide-and-recover performance on catalogue labels; it is not a leaderboard projection or rank predictor. KTH-88 does not demonstrate that it can beat H33 or the public best.
