Methodological boundaries

The 100 m grid and hidden faults make holdout transport uncertain. Label-derived features must be recomputed with only fold-visible faults and masked pixel-exactly; geology-only bands must not encode a catalogue label. This new score surface is label-blind, but it still lost to the incumbent.

The sample TIFF was restored from the owner's mirror, not authenticated with a registered account. The target is fault traces associated with potential geothermal resources, not confirmed vents or geothermal production.
