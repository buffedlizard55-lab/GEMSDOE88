Hypothesis & mechanism

Magnetic basement-fabric dislocation

Hypothesis: buried basement offsets leave a change in axial RTP fabric orientation on opposite sides of an untraced structure; an independent gravity gradient supports the interpretation when there is basement density contrast.

Mechanism: derive signed RTP gradients, smooth a structure tensor, measure paired-side axial orientation contrast, modulate by ranks of gravity and magnetic gradient energy, and select spaced directional crests. During holdout, only visible faults define the exclusion collar; hidden fault labels are not predictors.

Named non-fault mimics: unfaulted intrusive/volcanic lithologic contacts and aeromagnetic flight-line seams can rotate or interrupt apparent magnetic fabric.
