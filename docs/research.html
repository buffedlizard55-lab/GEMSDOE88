Ranked hypotheses · research shortlist only

Mechanisms to investigate after the blockers are resolved

The full review ranks four distinct candidates and names physical layers/transforms, off-catalogue mechanisms, non-fault mimics, prior-code/proposal overlap, qualitative DTI potential/cost, and access limits. These are not numeric score projections and were not implemented.

  1. Cross-geometry InSAR displacement discontinuity: Sentinel-1 ascending/descending LOS and coherence time series; test persistent geometric agreement. Mimics include pumping subsidence and seasonal swelling. High uncertainty, high data/processing cost; InSAR was previously proposed.
  2. Seasonal hydro-vegetation corridor persistence: multi-date Sentinel-2 NDVI/NDMI/NDWI anomalies. Mimics include irrigation, canals, playa moisture, and crop phenology. High uncertainty/cost; related concept previously proposed.
  3. Multi-year thermal anomaly persistence: season-matched Landsat surface temperature with structural corroboration. Mimics include emissivity, irrigation, wildfire, and illumination. Moderate uncertain potential/high cost; related concept previously proposed.
  4. Cross-catchment signed drainage deflection: χ/ksn and channel/knickpoint layers with replicated orientation across neighboring catchments. Mimics include lithology, base-level change, and engineering. Moderate uncertain potential/medium-high cost; builds on existing terrain methods and earlier proposals.

Blockers: the competition training feature stack and several derived drainage layers are missing; official external endpoints could not be reached here; access, area coverage, processing cost, and redistribution terms remain unverified; and the universal literal overlap rule must be reconciled before any non-empty raster generation.
