What H33's user-reported 0.2778 can—and cannot—tell us

The repository can verify H33's construction lineage, but it has no organizer receipt for 0.2778. H33 was formed by subtraction from an earlier raster: dots within two pixels of the visible catalogue were removed, leaving 37,654 positives and zero on-catalogue pixels. Under the asymmetric DTI objective, removing a true false-positive decreases the denominator's 0.2-weighted penalty without reducing triangular-kernel truth credit. Its roughly 300 m lattice spacing also limits redundant nearby predictions because truth credit takes a local maximum whereas false-positive mass accumulates. These mechanisms make the reported improvement geologically and metrically plausible.

They do not prove causality, hidden-test transfer, or the 0.2778 value. The score remains user-reported; the 0.2747 value in H33's source record is explicitly a projection. Correlations across the historical fleet are observational and confounded by shared ancestry and inputs. Only an actual organizer receipt could establish an organizer-confirmed score.
