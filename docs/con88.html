Why clearance was withdrawn

The supplied protocol defines dot support as positive predictions and requires no more than 70% of candidate dots to fall within 3 pixels of any registry raster. The SHA-pinned 17GEMSDOE raster is positive on every allowed pixel. Therefore, for every nonempty candidate, literal forward overlap is 1.0 > 0.70. The prior CON88 process substituted top-N equal-support pixels for that dense raster. That exception was not authorized by the protocol and is not used by the shared generation guard.

The surface-correlation check before placement was also not recorded for this artifact. The stored equal-support numbers, prior local format audit, and historical holdout diagnostics cannot cure either issue. No organizer submission receipt or score exists here.
