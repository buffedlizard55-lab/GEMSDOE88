Research rationale, with the causal caveat

Repository forensics describe H33 as a 37,654-dot strict subset of SOLO-D2.8 (40,199), D2.8 (44,090), and H19-5 (121,131), with no dots added along that lineage. The measured H33 lattice is spaced near the 3-pixel scoring-kernel radius, and reported pruning removed catalogue-near dots. Given the distance-weighted Tversky metric, removing a dot that earns negligible hidden-truth credit can lower false-positive cost with little recall loss. That is a plausible mechanism—not a proven causal explanation—because the hidden truth and a trusted H33 receipt are unavailable here.

The archived CON88 idea combined fleet consensus and a label-blind geophysical/topographic ridge. Shared inputs can create correlated false positives (lithologic contacts, shoreline benches, and survey seams), and the historical re-emission used visible-fault distance within folds but fixed precomputed consensus/ridge surfaces, so the full fold-local/per-feature-canary protocol record is incomplete. No claim that CON88 beats H33 or the public leaderboard is supported.
