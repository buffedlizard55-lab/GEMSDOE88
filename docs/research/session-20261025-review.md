These measurements establish a subtractive lineage and spacing/collar geometry. They do **not** establish that the removed dots had zero hidden-truth credit, that thinning caused the reported score increase, or that H33's original prediction method was a particular model. The evaluator's 300 m triangular-kernel DTI algebra makes it plausible that removing redundant/low-credit dots can reduce weighted false-positive mass while preserving truth coverage. Without hidden competition truth, that remains a mechanism hypothesis—not causal attribution.
