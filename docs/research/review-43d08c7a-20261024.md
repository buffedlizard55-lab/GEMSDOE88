## Why H33 may have performed well — corrected strength of conclusion

H33's reported 0.2778 and the prompt's differing leaderboard maxima are **user claims, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED receipts** in this review. Disk lineage supports a sparse-thinning / catalogue-collar explanation. Under the shared DTI definition, deleting a dot with zero marginal truth credit and positive false-positive weight improves the denominator without reducing the numerator. However, geometric proximity alone cannot prove that a removed dot had zero truth credit; newly mapped fault geometry can lie near known geometry. Not every neighboring dot is redundant, and thinning can reduce recall. Thus the measured subset relationship plus reported ordering is consistent with improved precision, **not a causal proof** or a numerical prediction of beating H33.
