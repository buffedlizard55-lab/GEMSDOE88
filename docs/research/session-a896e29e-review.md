## Why GEMSDOE32's listed `0.2778` may have performed well — plausible mechanism only

The `0.2778` is **unreceipted `USER-REPORTED CLAIM`**, not `HOLDOUT-DTI`, not a fresh score, and not `ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED`. The SHA-pinned H33-2-B2 geometry gives a plausible thinning explanation:

- D2.8 has 44,090 positive pixels; SOLO-D2.8 has 40,199; H33-2-B2 has 37,654. H33 is a strict positive-pixel subset of both parents.
- The 6,436 D2.8 pixels removed before H33 were all within 2 pixels (200 m) of the known-catalogue mask: 3,891 at or inside 1 pixel, 2,545 between 1 and 2 pixels; zero removed beyond 2 pixels. H33 has zero positive pixels within 2 pixels. Two read-only implementations—distance-transform lineage and independent Euclidean disk dilation—reproduced these counts from SHA-pinned files. See [lineage evidence](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE88/blob/arena/a896e29e-gemsdoe88/evidence/h33_lineage_20261028_a896e29e.json) and [independent collar cross-check](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE88/blob/arena/a896e29e-gemsdoe88/evidence/h33_collar_20261028_a896e29e.json).
- Under the documented DTI, each hidden truth pixel receives maximum triangular-kernel credit from nearby predictions (300 m radius), while prediction mass not credited to a truth pixel contributes false-positive weight (`α=0.2`; false negatives use `β=0.8`). Removing close, redundant or weakly credited dots **could** reduce penalty if their lost truth credit is small. This is a metric-consistent hypothesis, not a measured explanation.

The hidden labels and an organizer receipt for the submission are unavailable. Geometry cannot tell whether the deleted pixels were false positives, whether the reported value is accurate, or whether leaderboard selection/noise contributed. A stored fixed-H33 hide-and-recover value in an older record was explicitly marked **fold-unfair** and is not a valid H33 score or estimate; I do not substitute it here. No evidence supports claiming a new result above `0.2778`.
