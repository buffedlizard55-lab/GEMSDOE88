## H33 (`GEMSDOE32`, reported 0.2778): evidence-based explanation

The value **0.2778 is USER-REPORTED in this repository, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED**: no matching DrivenData receipt is stored, and the competition account was not accessed. The local forensic dossier (`research/h33-forensics-20261024.md`; data-level record `evidence/h33_forensics_20261024.json`) measures H33 as a strict pixel subset of SOLO-D2.8, itself a subset of D2.8 and H19-5. The recorded lineage removes dots rather than adding them; the final removals are near the known-fault catalogue, and the dot geometry has roughly 3-pixel nearest-neighbour spacing.

A plausible metric mechanism is the pooled distance-weighted Tversky structure: each truth pixel takes maximum nearby credit while each prediction dot contributes to the false-positive accounting. Redundant adjacent dots may add false-positive weight without adding much truth credit; deleting genuinely low-credit dots can therefore raise the score. The algebra and subset counts make this explanation consistent with the reported ordering. They do **not** establish that every removed point was a false positive or causally explain the organizer score: public truth is hidden, the score has no receipt here, and the files share lineage. H33’s observed properties are not evidence that a different raster will beat it.

The README quotes multiple leaderboard maxima from different dates (`0.3195` and `0.4413`); neither was live-verified in this sandbox. Do not describe either as the current leaderboard without fetching the official page. Beating any of these numbers is unproven; HOLDOUT-DTI is a different measurement and never a leaderboard score.
