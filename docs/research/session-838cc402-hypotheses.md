## 3. Detailed Physical & Statistical Specifications for Preregistration (Once `D1` Clears)

### 3.1 Rank #1 — `H88-SPRV`: Cross-Strike Hanks–Bucknam Scarp-Profile Restoration Likelihood
- **Governing Physics:** Under linear hillslope diffusion $\frac{\partial z}{\partial t} = \kappa \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2}$ (Wallace 1977; Bucknam & Anderson 1979; Hanks et al. 1984, *JGR*), an initially sharp normal-fault scarp of vertical throw $\Delta H$ on a planar alluvial fan of ambient slope $b$ evolves into:
  $$z(u; u_0, a, b, \Delta H, \tau) = a + b u + \frac{\Delta H}{2}\operatorname{erf}\!\left(\frac{u - u_0}{2\sqrt{\tau}}\right), \qquad \tau = \kappa t > 0.$$
  When $\sqrt{\tau} \gtrsim 100\text{–}250\text{ m}$ (pre-Holocene rupture or rapid loess burial), single-cell slope $|\partial z/\partial u|$ drops into background piedmont noise, causing reconnaissance fault compilers to omit the trace while the multi-pixel profile integral across $u \in [-800, +800]\text{ m}$ retains high signal-to-noise ratio.
- **Leakage & Canary Safeguards:** Strictly zero `existing_faults.tif` or `trace_segments_utm11.csv` inputs in the `SPRV` feature block; every single channel tested alone on `gems57-pooled-hide-v2` with mandatory `symmetric AUC <= 0.90` gate before model fitting.
- **Allocator De-Duplication (`< 0.70` vs `HGVC`/`BPWD`):** Rather than feeding `PHYS-44 + VIS-23` into the shared greedy crest allocator, `SPRV` emits its blind/piedmont quota directly along connected restored scarp inflection curves $u = u_0(x)$ where $\text{LLR}(x)$ exceeds fold-calibrated thresholds and both fluvial (`chan_n`) and shoreline (`Var_t(zmean_m)`) vetoes clear.

### 3.2 Rank #2 — `H88-PGTD`: 3D Laplace-Consistent Marussi Tensor Dimensionality & Euler Index
- **Governing Physics:** Every potential field $\Phi$ (`iso_grav_anom`, `rtp`) in source-free upper half-space $z > 0$ obeys $\nabla^2 \Phi = 0$, so its symmetric $3\times 3$ second-derivative tensor $\mathbf{T} = \nabla\nabla\Phi$ is trace-free ($\operatorname{tr}\mathbf{T} = 0$) and has real eigenvalues $\lambda_1 + \lambda_2 + \lambda_3 = 0$ (Pedersen & Rasmussen 1990, *Geophysics*; Beiki & Pedersen 2010, *Geophysics*). Over an ideal 2D dipping fault step, one horizontal eigenvalue vanishes ($\lambda_{\text{strike}} = 0 \implies \det\mathbf{T} = 0$, dimensionality index $I = 0$), whereas 3D volcanic plugs, calderas, and equant plutons exhibit $|\det\mathbf{T}| / \|\mathbf{T}\|_F^3 > 0$. Combining $I \to 0$ with Reid et al. (1990) Euler structural index $N = \frac{(x - x_0)T_{xz} + (y - y_0)T_{yz} + (z - z_0)T_{zz}}{\partial \Phi / \partial z} \in [0, 0.5]$ separates sub-alluvial 2D fault steps ($N \approx 0$) from 2D dikes ($N \approx 1$) and 3D plugs ($N \ge 2$).

### 3.3 Rank #3 — `H88-CBBC`: Conductive Basin-Breach Saddle Topology
- **Governing Physics:** Hydrothermal smectite/illite clay alteration caps lower electrical resistivity by $1\text{–}2$ orders of magnitude (`B17 cond_surf`). When high surface conductivity is caused by closed-basin evaporite playas, its spatial support is confined to bathymetric depocenters (`B15 depth_to_base_surf` maxima, `B13 iso_grav_anom` minima). Conversely, when hydrothermal fluids ascend along transverse relay faults or horst-bounding step-overs, conductive ridges connect across structural basement highs (topological 1-saddles of `depth_to_base_surf` / `iso_grav_anom`) at high angle to basin-depth contours.
