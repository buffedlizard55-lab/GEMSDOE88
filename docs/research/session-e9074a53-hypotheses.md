## Detailed Physical & Algorithmic Specifications

### 1. `H88-ANIS` — Anisotropic Cauchy Potential-Field Dip-Plane Surface-Trace Extrapolator (Rank #1, Zero External Download)

- **Exact input layers:**
  - `data/training_features.tif`: `B01` (complete Bouguer/isostatic gravity), `B02` (RTP magnetics), `B03` (depth-to-basement $Z_b$), `B10` (gravity horizontal gradient magnitude), `B11` (TMI horizontal gradient magnitude), `B12` & `B19` (DEM elevation/slope).
  - `data/external/05_3dep_10m_topo/` and `data/external/09_3dep_1m_lidar/` (surface micro-scarp verification gate at the extrapolated $z=0$ locus).
- **Physical signature & mathematical transform:**
  - Normal faults in the Great Basin dip at $\delta \in [45^\circ, 65^\circ]$ (`INGENIOUS` structural synthesis). Consequently, upward-continued gravity and RTP horizontal-gradient "worms" (`H88-BPWD` / `H88-HGVC`) migrate **downdip into the hanging wall** with increasing continuation height $z$: for a dipping density/susceptibility contact extending from surface depth $d_{\text{top}}$ to basement $Z_b$, the wavelet modulus maximum at continuation altitude $z$ sits at horizontal offset
    $$x_{\text{crest}}(z) = x_{\text{surface}} + (z + d_{\text{top}})\cot\delta\,\mathbf{\hat{n}}(\psi),$$
    which shifts `BPWD` and `HGVC` crests **400–2,000 m (`4–20 px`) off the surface fault trace**—completely outside the `300 m` (`3 px`) linear-decay DTI evaluation kernel!
  - `H88-ANIS` applies an **anisotropic 2D directional Cauchy wavelet bank** across $K = 8$ strike orientations $\psi_k \in [0, \pi)$ and $M = 5$ upward-continuation heights $z_m \in \{200, 400, 800, 1600, 3200\}\text{ m}$ in 2D FFT space:
    $$\widehat{\Psi}_{\psi_k, z_m}(\mathbf{k}) = (\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{\hat{n}}_k)^2 \exp(-|\mathbf{k}| z_m)\,\mathbf{1}_{\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{\hat{n}}_k > 0}\exp\!\left(-\frac{(\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{\hat{t}}_k)^2}{2\sigma_t^2 |\mathbf{k}|^2}\right).$$
  - Linking cross-scale modulus maxima across $z_1 < z_2 < \dots < z_5$ along local fault-normal $\mathbf{\hat{n}}_k$ yields a 5-point $(z_m, s_m)$ ridge trajectory with weighted least-squares slope $\hat{\beta}_1 = \widehat{\cot\delta}$ and **surface intercept $\hat{s}_0 = x_{\text{crest}}(z \to 0^+)$**, weighted by gravity–magnetic cross-wavelet phase coherence $|\langle W^{\text{grav}}_{\psi_k,z_m} \overline{W^{\text{mag}}_{\psi_k,z_m}}\rangle|$ and gated by `B03` basement-step sign consistency ($\nabla Z_b \cdot \mathbf{\hat{n}}_k > 0$ in the downdip direction).
  - Probability mass is transported from the hanging-wall worm centroids $(\bar{x}, \bar{y})$ directly onto the extrapolated $z = 0$ surface rupture locus $\hat{s}_0$, followed by skeletonized spacing (`min_d_cat >= 3.0 px`, `min_nn >= 2.83 px`).
- **Why it catches off-catalogue faults missed by USGS/INGENIOUS:**
  - Intrabasin and piedmont normal faults buried under thin (`10–80 m`) Holocene fan veneer lack continuous meter-scale scarps in airphoto compilations (missing from Qfaults), while raw potential-field gradient worms (`BPWD`, `PSGV`, `GZZC`) place dots above the mid-crustal hanging-wall density step (`0.5–2 km` basinward of where an expert mapper draws the surface/near-surface fault trace). Extrapolating the multi-altitude Cauchy trajectory back to $z=0$ lands directly within `<= 300 m` (`3 px`) of the true subcrop trace.
- **Named non-fault geological mimic & rejection filter:**
  - **Tilted Tertiary ash-flow tuff / basalt hogbacks, caldera ring margins, and pluton aureoles** produce coherent dipping magnetic contacts without Quaternary faulting. Rejected by requiring (i) gravity–magnetic **cross-wavelet co-linearity** with downward-normal basement deepening ($\nabla Z_b \cdot \mathbf{\hat{n}}_k > 0$, eliminating perched volcanic mesas where $Z_b$ does not step) and (ii) absence of closed circular azimuth Loop closure characteristic of pluton/caldera margins.
- **How it differs from existing repo lanes:**
  - `src/gems88/bpwd.py` and `src/gems88/psgv.py` use **isotropic** upward continuation and stack worm density directly at the **unshifted altitude centroids**; `src/gems88/hgvc.py` shifts shallow MT/radiometric sinter **updip** heuristically via gravity gradient sign without fitting a multi-altitude Cauchy dip plane $(x_0, \cot\delta)$ or extrapolating potential-field crests to $z=0$.
- **Leakage check:** Zero catalogue-derived features in the Cauchy scale-space operator (`d_cat` used solely as post-prediction collar `> 2.83 px`); single-feature symmetric holdout AUC expected `< 0.65` (`<= 0.90` gate).

- **Physical signature & mathematical transform:**
  - Hydrothermal fluids circulating along active normal fault cores oxidize primary titanomagnetite to hematite/pyrite/clays (`magnetite destruction`), creating narrow (`30–120 m` wide) negative short-wavelength magnetic susceptibility notches flanked by host-rock susceptibility steps, plus narrow K/Th radiometric spikes where fluids vent.

  - `H88-FLDC` runs a **drape-altitude-normalized 1D analytical-signal & fault-gouge notch matched filter** directly on the raw `090°` flight-line fiducial `.csv` profiles using exact per-sample radar clearance $h_{\text{drape}}(s)$, links detections across $\ge 3$ adjacent E–W flight lines along physically admissible Basin-and-Range strikes (`|\Delta x / \Delta y| \le \tan 55^\circ`), and rasterizes the linked sub-grid fault traces onto the `100 m` `EPSG:32611` competition grid.
- **Why it catches off-catalogue faults missed by USGS/INGENIOUS:**
  - Concealed intrabasin faults beneath young playa/eolian sheets and range-front splay strands lacking preserved Holocene scarps still host `40–100 m` oxidized magnetite-destruction damage zones resolved at `~5 m` along-line sampling.

- **How it differs from existing repo lanes:**

### 3. `H88-FSTD` — Zero-Catalogue 3D Tensorial Mohr–Coulomb Slip & Dilation Tendency (`T_s`, `T_d`) on Potential-Field Fault Planes (Rank #3)

- **Official verified external sources:**
  - DOE GDR `#1391` (`geodetics_INGENIOUS_regional_data.zip`, `doi:10.15121/1881483`, [https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391), **CC-BY-4.0**) providing full 2D horizontal GPS strain-rate tensor components $(\dot{\varepsilon}_{EE}, \dot{\varepsilon}_{NN}, \dot{\varepsilon}_{EN})$ and principal extension azimuth $\theta_{\text{SHmin}}$, plus USGS Great Basin Ambient Crustal Stress (`doi:10.5066/P9YL58W6`, [https://doi.org/10.5066/P9YL58W6](https://doi.org/10.5066/P9YL58W6), Siler 2022, **CC0** — using **only** regional stress-ratio $R = (\sigma_1 - \sigma_2)/(\sigma_1 - \sigma_3)$ and $S_{H\max}$ azimuth grids, explicitly **excluding** its Qfaults-derived fault shapefile to preserve zero catalogue leakage).
- **Physical signature & mathematical transform:**
  - Critically stressed fractures control crustal permeability in the Great Basin (Morris, Ferrill & Henderson 1996; Faulds & Hinz 2015). Given principal effective stresses $\sigma'_1 > \sigma'_2 > \sigma'_3$ oriented by the geodetic strain-rate eigenvectors, a candidate fault plane with unit normal $\mathbf{n}(\phi, \delta)$ estimated **purely from potential-field tilt-derivative strike $\phi(x,y)$ and Salem (2007) magnetic/gravity tilt-gradient dip $\delta(x,y)$** experiences normal and shear tractions:
    $$\sigma_n = \mathbf{n}^\top \boldsymbol{\sigma}' \mathbf{n}, \qquad \tau = \sqrt{\|\boldsymbol{\sigma}'\mathbf{n}\|^2 - \sigma_n^2}, \qquad T_s = \frac{\tau}{\sigma_n}, \qquad T_d = \frac{\sigma'_1 - \sigma_n}{\sigma'_1 - \sigma'_3}.$$
  - `H88-FSTD` scores candidate potential-field discontinuities by combined fracture reactivation capacity $J_{\text{crit}} = T_s \cdot T_d \cdot C_{\text{coh}}$, peaking on NNE-striking moderately dipping planes under WNW–ESE extension and on localized transtensional Walker Lane step-overs.
- **Why it catches off-catalogue faults missed by USGS/INGENIOUS:**
  - `src/gems88/sdtl.py` (`H88-SDTL`) failed in blind terrain (`HOLDOUT-DTI = 0.0897`) because **9 of its 12 features (`sdtl.py:21–34`) were computed from `visible_catalogue` fault segments** (`cat_slip_tendency`, `cat_dilation_tendency`, `cat_Combined_ts_td`, `cat_tip_wing_stress`, `cat_relay_overlap`, `cat_dist_km`), which decay exponentially to zero beyond a few kilometers from already-mapped faults! By deriving $(\phi, \delta)$ from geophysical potential fields and $(\theta_{\text{SHmax}}, R)$ from GPS geodesy, `H88-FSTD` has **100% spatial support across unmapped `>25 px` basins**.
- **Named non-fault geological mimic & rejection filter:**
  - **Mesozoic thrust ramps, low-angle metamorphic core-complex detachment surfaces, and granitic joint sets** that happen to strike NNE. Rejected by 3D dip discrimination: low-angle thrusts ($\delta < 25^\circ$) and sub-vertical cooling joints ($\delta > 82^\circ$) yield suppressed normal-faulting $T_s$ under Andersonian extensional stress ($\sigma_1 = \sigma_v$).
- **How it differs from existing repo lanes:**
  - `sdtl.py` projects scalar `B04/B07/B08` proxies onto **catalogue** strikes; `ssa.py` uses scalar azimuth coherence without full vector GPS principal axes or 3D dip $\delta(x,y)$ Mohr–Coulomb traction $(T_s, T_d)$.

### 4. `H88-CHFR` — Convective Heat-Flow Departure & Péclet Upflow-Plume Adjoint Back-Tracing (Rank #4)

- **Official verified external sources:**
  - USGS Great Basin Heat Flow & Thermal Models (`doi:10.5066/P9BZPVUC`, [https://doi.org/10.5066/P9BZPVUC](https://doi.org/10.5066/P9BZPVUC), DeAngelo et al. 2022, **CC0 Public Domain**) + USGS 3D ModEM Magnetotelluric Conductance Depth Slices (`doi:10.5066/P9TWT2LU`, [https://doi.org/10.5066/P9TWT2LU](https://doi.org/10.5066/P9TWT2LU), Peacock & Bedrosian 2022, **CC0 Public Domain**).
- **Physical signature & mathematical transform:**
  - `training_features.tif` `B09` contains smooth regional conductive heat flow, which blurs localized hydrothermal upflow across tens of kilometers. In reality, blind geothermal systems (75% of Great Basin resources lack surface hot springs) manifest in temperature-gradient wells as **positive convective heat-flow departures** $\Delta q_{\text{conv}, i} = q_{\text{well}, i} - q_{\text{LOESS}}(\mathbf{x}_i) > +25\text{ mW m}^{-2}$ that spread laterally down the shallow water-table gradient (`lateral outflow plumes`).
  - `H88-CHFR` solves the steady advection–diffusion adjoint equation backward (up-topographic/piezometric gradient) from positive $\Delta q_{\text{conv}}$ anomalies until intersecting a subsurface structural permeability boundary defined by coupled `B10` gravity HGM + deep (`2–15 km`) ModEM MT conductance gradient (`P9TWT2LU`), concentrating fault probability on the **up-gradient feeder fault** rather than the shifted shallow thermal well halo.
- **Why it catches off-catalogue faults missed by USGS/INGENIOUS:**
  - Industry temperature-gradient drilling targeted blind thermal anomalies in alluvial basins (e.g., Desert Peak, Salt Wells, McGinness Hills) where Quaternary fault traces were unmapped prior to drilling; adjoint plume back-tracing pinpoints the concealed feeder fault step.
- **Named non-fault geological mimic & rejection filter:**
  - **Radiogenic high-U/Th Mesozoic/Tertiary granitoids and thick low-thermal-conductivity diatomite/playa clay blankets** (which elevate conductive gradient $\partial T/\partial z$ without fault-hosted fluid advection). Rejected by subtracting radiogenic heat generation estimated from `B17–B19` (U, Th, K) and requiring deep (`5–15 km`) sub-vertical MT conductance roots (`P9TWT2LU`) rather than shallow flat-lying basin-fill smectite caps (`0–2 km` slice).
- **How it differs from existing repo lanes:**

### 5. `H88-NWIS-HB` — Piezometric Hydraulic-Head Step & Fault-Core Aquitard Baffle Detector (Rank #5)

- **Official verified external source:**
  - USGS Water Data OGC API v0 (`https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/ogcapi/v0/collections/monitoring-locations` and `field-measurements`, **CC0 / U.S. Public Domain**; verified active replacement for legacy `https://waterservices.usgs.gov/`, which sunsets `2027-02-22`).
- **Physical signature & mathematical transform:**
  - Even where agricultural leveling, eolian sand sheets, or active playa sedimentation completely obliterate meter-scale surface scarps in 3DEP lidar (`H88-LSC`), Quaternary normal faults cutting unconsolidated basin fill develop clay-smeared, mineralized **low-permeability fault-core gouge zones** flanked by fractured damage-zone conduits (Bense et al. 2013). Across-strike groundwater flow produces sharp **potentiometric head steps** (`5–40 m` water-table drops over `< 500 m` horizontal distance) and abrupt discontinuities in seasonal pumping-drawdown response.
  - `H88-NWIS-HB` harvests static pre-pumping groundwater head elevations $h_i = z_{\text{DEM}}(\mathbf{x}_i) - \text{DTW}_i$ across the Nevada/Utah/Oregon/Idaho study footprint, computes directional hydraulic-gradient modulus $|\nabla h_{\text{KED}}|$ normalized by topographic slope $|\nabla z_{\text{DEM}}|$ (`excess hydraulic resistance`), and intersects high-resistance barriers with `B10` gravity basement steps.
- **Why it catches off-catalogue faults missed by USGS/INGENIOUS:**
  - Directly senses subsurface **hydraulic compartmentalization** inside flat agricultural/playa valleys (`> 25 px` from range-front catalogue traces) where optical, lidar, and radiometric surface expressions are zero.
- **Named non-fault geological mimic & rejection filter:**
  - **Agricultural center-pivot pumping cones of depression and alluvial-fan/playa-margin permeability pinch-outs.** Rejected by (i) multi-decade median winter static water-level aggregation + well-screen depth filtering to suppress seasonal pumping cones and (ii) requiring linear strike continuity (`>= 2 km`) co-aligned with sub-alluvial `B10/B11` potential-field gradients rather than concentric closed drawdown contours.
- **How it differs from existing repo lanes:**
  - Zero modules or prior hypotheses in `GEMSDOE88` use hydrogeologic potentiometric head data (`NWIS` / USGS Water Data OGC API).
