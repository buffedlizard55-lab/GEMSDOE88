## Three conditional hypotheses, ranked before any implementation

These are the existing source-fingerprinted `registry/hypothesis_queue.json` proposals, not newly demonstrated discoveries. Their novelty is relative to reviewed implementations in this checkout, not the whole field or parallel fleet. Costs and ordering are qualitative; there are **no projected numerical scores**. All are blocked before feature construction by the preflight.

1. **H88-SPR — repeated scarp-profile restoration (medium cost).** Inputs: cached detrended elevation/slope, pinned 10 m-derived terrain indices, LiDAR step/strike/valid bands, and drainage masks. Fit smooth landform plus signed step versus a no-step model on separate along-strike swaths; hold out profile samples. A repeatable subdued step could mark a fault absent from the public catalogue, unlike existing pointwise scarp/knickpoint responses. Named nulls: river terrace risers and pluvial shorelines; reject if channel/contour controls explain the same response or signs do not repeat. No claim that a subpixel scarp is resolved by the 100 m grid.
2. **H88-AWA — terrain-matched radiometric alteration-width asymmetry (medium cost).** Inputs: quantized GeoDAWN K/Th/U indices, RTP magnetics, basement depth and slope. Compare within-band residual profile decay widths across an independent magnetic depression; a covered permeable structure might create asymmetry without a mapped scarp. Different from existing level/edge features. Named nulls: weathered lithologic contacts and K-rich transported alluvium. Reject if terrain/parent-material or quantization-matched controls reproduce the asymmetry; encoded indices are not calibrated element concentrations or physical K/Th ratios.
3. **H88-CBC — conductive basin-breach connectivity (medium–high cost).** Inputs: detrended elevation, isostatic gravity, depth-to-basement and conductivity surfaces plus drainage-validity masks. Compare cross-boundary conductivity connectivity and gravity discontinuity against a smooth basin-fill model and drainage geometry; a buried structural connection may be off-catalogue. Unlike conductivity curvature or thermal conjunctions, the proposed contrast is topological. Named nulls: unfaulted permeable paleochannels and saline groundwater plumes. Reject if those nulls match the bridge or independent gravity support is absent; coarse conductivity cannot resolve a metre-scale conduit.

The older five-proposal dossier now carries a correction: its numerical DTI projections are withdrawn, its signed 2-D cross-gradient/strike expression is invalid as written, its scalar strain bands do not determine an extension direction, and surface topography is not generally hydraulic head. No hypothesis was fitted.
