Context

Why the fleet best (0.2778, user-reported) works — and what beating it requires

Recomputed from hash-verified rasters: the 0.2778 file is not a new model but nested false-positive removal — H19-5 ridge support (121,131 dots, 0.1922) thinned to kernel-matched spacing (44,090, 0.2600), then a 2 px catalogue collar deleting 6,436 near-catalogue dots that earn almost no credit under live masking (→ 37,654, 0.2778). Lineage arithmetic implies it still covers only ~37–40% of public truth credit; reaching the reported leaderboard top (0.4413) needs roughly +60% covered credit, i.e. new, precisely localised support where the ridge lineage is blind (low-relief buried basin faults). MDPS-88 tested exactly that support with matched-domain training; it improved far-subset efficiency per dot (1.2–3.1×) but not decisively (paired CI crosses zero), and its blind SGMC geologic-map proxy (0.0667) trails H33 (0.0940). Verdict: negative result, kept as a deliverable. DO NOT SUBMIT.
