Why H33 appeared strongest among the supplied projects

More credit per emitted pixel, not evidence of a proven geothermal vent.

The H33 source page documents a 37,654-dot file made by pruning dots within two 100 m pixels of previously mapped faults from an earlier prediction. The prompt reports 0.2778 for that file, but no corresponding submission-page receipt is available here; treat it as an unverified owner report.

The organizer's distance-weighted Tversky definition credits each truth pixel by its best nearby prediction while penalizing the total unmatched prediction mass. Sparse, well-spaced dots can therefore avoid paying repeatedly for redundant neighbouring pixels. Excluding near-catalogue predictions can help if those pixels do not cover new ground truth; it is not a causal proof that H33 won, nor does it predict performance on hidden faults.

Can this experiment beat H33's reported 0.2778 or the public leader? Not established: it lost our holdout comparison, no organizer score exists for it, and we did not consume a weekly slot. See historical experiments and official sources.
