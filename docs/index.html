Why the fleet best (0.2778, user-reported) works — and what beating it requires

Lineage arithmetic implies it still covers only ~37–40% of public truth credit; reaching the reported leaderboard top (0.4413) needs roughly +60% covered credit, i.e. new, precisely localised support where the ridge lineage is blind (low-relief buried basin faults).
