Why user-reported H33 reached 0.2778

The 0.2778 value remains a user report, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED, because no authenticated portal receipt exists here. Exact raster decomposition nevertheless supports a clear mechanism. H33 retained the 37,654-dot off-catalogue support of D2.8 while deleting exactly 6,436 near-catalogue flank dots within 200 m. Under DTI, false positives cost α=0.2; removing redundant, already-mapped flanks can reduce denominator penalty without sacrificing the distant range-front and piedmont hypotheses that carry hidden-fault recall.

Its sparse, spaced off-catalogue geometry is therefore scientifically consistent with a higher hidden score.
