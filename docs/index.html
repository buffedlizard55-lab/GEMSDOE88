Exact marginal DTI theorem

Let T be weighted hidden-truth coverage, M prediction-to-truth proximity credit, S total emitted prediction mass, and |G| hidden truth count (`α = 0.2, β = 0.8`):

DTI = T / [0.2 × (T + S − M) + 0.8 × |G|]

Adding one isolated off-catalogue unit dot with expected marginal coverage `ΔT` and own proximity credit `m_self` increases `DTI` if and only if:

ΔT > [0.2 × DTI / (1 − 0.2 × DTI)] × (1 − m_self)
