Provenance and access constraints

What was not authenticated

  • Private organizer test faults, private leaderboard results, and a receipt for H33's reported 0.2778.
  • The competition feature stack in this checkout; its owner mirror is not present and reuse/redistribution terms are not authenticated.
  • Exact-area Sentinel/Landsat/SAR scenes, quality masks, well coverage, raw GeoDAWN scale metadata, and derived-data sharing permission.
  • Organizer acceptance of any TIFF. Local format-validity must not be described as submit approval.

Source availability is not candidate viability

Public catalogs establish that official data products exist. A viable external-data experiment also needs exact footprint coverage, fit-for-purpose spatial/temporal resolution, reproducible retrieval, calibration, and permission to share derived data with the organizer.

Until those checks pass, external-data hypotheses remain proposals, not runnable lanes.
