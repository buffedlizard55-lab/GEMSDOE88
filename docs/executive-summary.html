PhD forensics: why H33 (0.2778) led its siblings

Why H33 rose — and can it be beaten?

0.2778 is user-reported, not an organizer score. No portal receipt exists in this repository for H33, so it can never be labeled ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED and cannot be used as a beatable benchmark.

What H33 is (measured on the pinned files)

  • Strict subset chain with no additions: H19-5 → D1.5 → D2.8 → SOLO-D2.8 → H33. Every reported gain comes from removing dots (6,436 dots within 2 px of the catalogue removed at the H33 step).
  • Metric algebra (pooled DTI, α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangular kernel): removing a dot raises DTI only if its marginal truth credit is below the bar ≈ 0.0556 at DTI 0.2778. Thinning clustered/redundant dots improves the precision–coverage trade-off.
  • H33 dots sit ~1 kernel radius apart (median spacing 3.0 px), which the max-vs-sum DTI structure rewards. Smallest distance to any mapped fault: 2.24 px; 0% within 2 px; median 19.6 px.
  • No single band explains it (best single-band AUC 0.657 on detrended slope). It is a multi-modal ridge product, not a one-band threshold.

Can a new submission beat 0.2778 / 0.4413?

No defensible guarantee either way. The public leaderboard top is 0.4413 and rank 50 is 0.2818, so H33's reported 0.2778 would sit below rank 50. Closing the gap to the first-round cut (~0.3539) needs roughly +27–29% more truth credit (~6–7k weighted pixels); closing to 0.4413 needs ~+59–63%. Sparsity alone does not prove hidden-truth recall is preserved. Only a DrivenData submission receipt can establish a score.

3–5 new, untried geological hypotheses (ranked)

Contrarian candidates worth testing before any slot is spent

Each names its layers, physical signature, why it could catch a fault missing from the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue, its non-fault mimic, and how it differs from implemented lanes. None is authorized to run while DUPLICATE_STOP binds; none has a numerical DTI projection (projections are never written as scores).

  1. H1 — Radiometric K alteration on young alluvial cover (this build)

    Layers: GeoDAWN eK + Th/K contrast; topo slope_p90 (young-cover proxy); DEM chan_n + knick_n (drainage control). Signature: elevated potassium metasomatism + low-slope cover that buries scarps + structurally controlled drainage. Why off-catalogue: young alluvium hides the scarp, so USGS/INGENIOUS never mapped the trace. Mimic: K-rich transported alluvium / unfaulted lithologic K source. Differs: this is the generated GEORAD artifact above.

  2. H2 — Repeated scarp-profile restoration vs drainage/shoreline null

    Layers: training_features det_elev/det_elev_slope; topo hs_lineament/dem_mean; lidar step_max/coh100/strike; DEM chan_n/valid_n. Signature: smooth-landform-plus-signed-step profile fit repeated across withheld swaths. Why off-catalogue: weak, cover-softened offsets on smooth piedmont escape mapping. Mimic: fluvial terrace risers / pluvial shoreline benches. Differs: fits a cross-validated landform-restoration likelihood.

  3. H3 — Terrain-matched radiometric alteration-width asymmetry

    Layers: GeoDAWN K/Th/U; training_features rtp/depth_to_base_surf/det_elev_slope. Signature: relative cross-strike decay-width asymmetry of terrain-matched residual profiles beside a local RTP depression. Why off-catalogue: a covered permeable structure alters one wall more broadly with no discrete scarp. Mimic: differential weathering of adjacent units / K-rich transported alluvium. Differs: tests fitted decay width, not another radiometric high.

  4. H4 — Conductive basin-breach connectivity vs paleochannel null

    Layers: training_features det_elev/iso_grav_anom/depth_to_base_surf/cond_surf; DEM chan_n/valid_n. Signature: narrow conductivity connectivity across basin-boundary saddles with a cross-boundary gravity discontinuity. Why off-catalogue: buried cross-basin connection may have no mapped surface trace. Mimic: unfaulted permeable alluvial paleochannels / saline plumes. Differs: topology-preserving cross-basin connectivity vs explicit drainage null.

  5. H5 — Blind normal-fault damage-zone low-relief alignment (no scarp)

    Layers: GeoDAWN TC + UTh; topo relief_local/curv_prof_absmax; lidar ex_max/step_max; dem10 chi. Signature: subtle low-relief linear alignments of radiometric total-count and curvature with no scarp expression. Why off-catalogue: no surface rupture → nothing to map. Mimic: relict alluvial fan margins / lithologic contacts. Differs: explicitly targets absence of scarp expression.

All five require the shared buffered whole-fault-segment holdout (gems57-pooled-hide-v2, α=0.2 β=0.8, 300 m triangular kernel), single-feature leakage canaries (AUC ≤ 0.90), and the full registry surface/dot gate before any weekly slot. H1 is implemented above as a research artifact; H2–H5 remain untested.
