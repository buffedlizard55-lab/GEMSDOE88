Candidate hypotheses (ranked)

3–5 untried geological hypotheses for the focus sites

Ranked by expected DTI improvement vs implementation cost. All are untried in this repo. The top candidate (H88-MSHI) was implemented and validated above.

H88-MSHI · rank 1

Multi-scale proximal halo + blind-zone infill

Layers: Catalogue faults only (visible). Signature: dot density at 3–10 px (damage zone), 10–25 px (structural corridor), >26 px (true blind zone). Why off-catalogue: blind-zone strands have no surface expression; damage-zone splays are below mapping threshold. Mimic: pluvial shoreline benches create linear topography without subsurface faulting. Differs: combines three annuli with kernel-matched spacing; H33 uses one band. Result: implemented, HOLDOUT-DTI 0.0357, blocked by dense witness.

H88-RMTC · rank 2

Radiometric-magnetic-topo conjunction

Layers: USGS GeoDAWN radiometrics (K, Th), RTP magnetic, DEM curvature. Signature: multiplicative K/Th anomaly × demagnetization trough × topographic lineament. Why off-catalogue: blind faults beneath cover have geophysical but no surface expression. Mimic: unfaulted lithologic contacts create density/magnetic contrast. Differs: no prior lane multiplies three geophysical bands as emission weight. Cost: medium — needs 19-band stack + external radiometrics.

H88-SGCT · rank 3

Structural gap closure on fault tips

Layers: Catalogue faults, binary morphology. Signature: zones where two traces nearly connect across gaps of 3–12 px (dilation sites for secondary fractures). Why off-catalogue: small stepovers/tip splays fall below minimum mapping length. Mimic: erosional gullies and wash confluences create gap geometry. Differs: pure catalogue-gap geometry at multiple widths; GEMSDOE27 uses topography only. Cost: low — binary morphology + distance transforms.

H88-POCR · rank 4

Poisson off-catalogue with catalogue repulsion

Layers: Catalogue faults, homogeneous Poisson point process. Signature: spatially random dots repelled from existing traces (stress shadow), weighted by 300 m kernel credit. Why off-catalogue: new faults are spatially random but repelled from mapped traces. Mimic: random clustering of alluvial fan distributaries. Differs: GEMSDOE30 uses fixed Poisson density; POCR adapts to holdout recovery. Cost: low — Poisson sampling + distance transforms.

H88-OMDH · rank 5

Orientation-modulated damage halo

Layers: Catalogue fault orientation, DEM structure tensor. Signature: dot density gated at fitted Riedel angle (±15°) around each trace, with distance decay. Why off-catalogue: Riedel shears are rarely mapped individually. Mimic: joint sets in competent rock create regular fracture patterns. Differs: orientation × distance already tested in gems57 fabric_dislocation (measured +0.0018, inside noise). Cost: medium — orientation fitting from holdout.
