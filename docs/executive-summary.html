How a future submission becomes eligible

  1. Record the assigned geological lane, immutable cached features, catalogue/grid provenance, full registry and current holdout incumbent.
  2. Run feature-alone leakage canaries. Any AUC above 0.90 blocks the experiment pending an explanation and audit.
  3. Withhold whole fault segments with buffers and recompute all catalogue-derived features from visible faults. Mask visible faults exactly. Evaluate pooled distance-weighted Tversky with the shared evaluator; report evaluator version, withheld positives, and spatial 95% confidence intervals.
  4. Show paired improvement over the incumbent. Stop after three experiments or two hours; do not tune against hidden leaderboard scores.
  5. Compare both the surface and final dots to every registry raster. Duplicate thresholds bind before packaging.
  6. Use the unmodified shared submission writer and independent on-disk validator. Record file SHA-256, a unique name and a note of at most 140 characters.
  7. A separate selector checks the current submission-page weekly cap. Passing local gates does not authorize a slot.

Avoid the “[0, 1]” rejection

Negative values, infinities, nodata sentinels, and values greater than one must not appear in predictions. Do not fix an unvalidated model by blindly clipping its outputs. The shared writer rejects invalid input and validates serialization. Our additional validator reads raw pixels so nodata masks cannot conceal invalid predictions.

python scripts/validate_artifact.py candidate.tif --template data/sample_submission.tif

This reports format only—not scientific validity, registry originality, organizer acceptance or submission approval. Actual competition validation remains authoritative.

Upload steps—only after separate slot approval

  1. Download the validated named TIFF from the submission desk and compare its SHA-256 to its receipt.
  2. Open the competition submissions page, sign in, and check the rules, current cap and format requirements.
  3. Choose the approved single-band .tif (or an archive containing only that TIFF), paste the associated short note, and submit.
  4. Save the organizer receipt. Only a result copied from that receipt may be labeled ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED.

These upload instructions follow the user-supplied form description; the submission page was not accessible from this sandbox. No upload automation or credentials are stored.
