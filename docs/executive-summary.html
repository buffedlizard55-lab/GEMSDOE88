How to submit a future cleared candidate

These instructions are conditional. They do not authorize uploading KTH-88, the quarantined synthetic MAG-PSG file, or any other historical raster.

  1. Require a green current-candidate gate

    Run the candidate-specific validator and require its `--require-submit` check to exit 0. Verify the current feed binds to the exact TIFF/ZIP SHA-256 and records `ok_to_submit: true`. Format validity alone is insufficient.

  2. Obtain a separate selector decision

    Confirm that the chosen method passed its buffered spatial holdout, leakage canaries, and both registry checks. A person or portfolio selector must separately authorize spending a weekly slot; the validator never spends one.

  3. Check the live organizer instructions

    Sign in to the team account and review the current official problem/format page, current rules, and live submission limit before uploading.

  4. Upload only the exact approved artifact

    Use the precise file whose hash appears in the approved run card. Enter its unique name and note verbatim (note length at most 140 characters). Do not substitute another local TIFF.

  5. Save the organizer receipt

    Retain the official submission receipt and copied score. Only a score shown in that receipt may be labelled ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED; a public leaderboard row or local holdout is not a receipt.
