What is required before a file can be marked ready?

  • Authenticated training features, labels, official sample raster, and permitted ancillary data.
  • The shared spatially blocked hide-and-recover evaluator and writer, with feature-only leakage checks.
  • A holdout result with evaluator version, withheld-positive count, and 95% confidence interval that beats the prior holdout best.
  • Pre-placement and final raster comparisons against the registry for correlation and dot overlap.
  • Successful format/range validation and an auditable manifest/hash. A holdout or projection is not an organizer-confirmed score.
