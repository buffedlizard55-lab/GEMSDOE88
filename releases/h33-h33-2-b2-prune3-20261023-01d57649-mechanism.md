## Non-fault process that could mimic the mechanism

In the refill set, alluvial channels, map-edge artifacts, and BFS geometry inside the binary H19-5 support can mimic fault lineaments without adding independent structural evidence. Denser refills can also change metric credit through redundancy rather than better geology.
