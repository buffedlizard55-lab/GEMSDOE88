## IR-88-22 — `AXIS = SNAP` Along-Strike Bunching Collapse vs `d_nn >= 2.8 px` Spacing Lock & $60^\circ$ Hanging-Wall Potential-Field Drift
- **Observed:** Naive unspaced `<= 2 px` window-argmax snapping (`min_sep = 0`) lowers blind off-catalogue USGS `SGMC` DTI from `R0 = 0.096060` to `0.091562 (-0.00450)` because adjacent `R0` dots along the same fault strand slide onto the same local gradient peak (`d_nn = 1.0 px`), opening `4–6 px` (`400–600 m > R = 300 m`) kernel coverage gaps along strike. Conversely, enforcing `R0`'s `d_nn >= 2.8 px` (`sqrt(8) = 2.8284 px`) inter-dot spacing during `<= 2.0 px` snapping restricts moves to across-strike crest centering and raises `SGMC` to **`0.096806 (+0.00075)`** on `SNAPscarp2px` (`fae77ff4`) and **`0.096439 (+0.00038)`** on `SNAPridge2px` (`83d2d231`). - **Decision Impact:** Enforced `min_sep = 2.8 px` (`min d_nn = 2.8284 px`), strictly provided `training_features.tif` bands (`Bands 3, 12, 18, 19`, zero un-supplied external LiDAR), and exact collar preservation (`2,171` `2–3 px` dots untouched; `100%` of moved dots at `d_cat > 3.0 px`) across all 3 `SNAP` variants (`scripts/build_snap_variants.py`, `tests/test_snap_axis.py`).
