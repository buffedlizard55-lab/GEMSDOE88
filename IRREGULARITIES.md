## IR-88-77 — The kernel-disjointness premise behind this lane is wrong: the official DTI charges false positives per DOT, not per unit of kernel area (2026-10-24)
- **Observed (preregistered hypothesis falsified, and the error was in the premise, not the code):** `KDMC-88` was preregistered on the argument that two dots closer than `2R = 6 px` share kernel mass, that the shared area is "paid for twice in `FP_w` and credited once in `TP_w`", and that a kernel-disjoint packing must therefore dominate. Reading `vendor/gems57/metric.py::dti_exact` line by line disproves the premise: `fp = sum_x p(x) * (1 - q(x))` where `q(x)` is the largest kernel weight from cell `x` to any truth pixel. The false-positive term is a sum over **emitted dots**, each charged `1 - k_self` once. It does not contain any term in the overlap between two dots' kernels. Kernel overlap between dots is free; only the dot count is charged. Symmetrically, `tp` credits each truth pixel with the **maximum** kernel weight over all dots, so a truth pixel gains nothing from a second nearby dot but loses everything if the nearest dot is `>= 3 px` away.

Mechanism: competition truth is one-pixel-wide lineaments, and at `6 px` spacing the midpoint of a line segment sits `3 px` from both neighbouring dots where `k(3) = 0`, so it earns nothing.
