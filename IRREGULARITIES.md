- **Decision Impact:** Kept Holdout-DTI strictly `REPORT-ONLY` alongside blind `SGMC` control and `12 km` block-density CV (`0.5590 -> 0.5700` inside `0.51–0.59`, rising to `0.5937` at `p4px`).

## IR-88-32 — `AXIS = SNAP` Along-Strike Bunching Collapse vs `d_nn >= 2.8 px` Spacing Lock & $60^\circ$ Hanging-Wall Potential-Field Drift
- **Observed:** Unspaced `<= 2 px` window-argmax snapping (`w2E1/S2/E2` and `g2f35/d2f35/d1f35`, `min_sep = 1.0 px`) lowers blind off-catalogue USGS `SGMC` DTI from `R0 = 0.096060` to `0.088098–0.09505` because adjacent dots along the same fault strand slide onto adjacent pixels (`d_nn = 1.0 px`), opening `4–6 px` (`400–600 m > R = 300 m`) kernel coverage gaps along strike. Conversely, enforcing `R0`'s `d_nn >= 2.8 px` (`sqrt(8) = 2.8284 px`) inter-dot spacing during `<= 2.0 px` snapping restricts moves to across-strike crest centering and raises `SGMC` to **`0.096806 (+0.00075)`** on `SNAPscarp2px` (`fae77ff4`) and **`0.096439 (+0.00038)`** on `SNAPridge2px` (`83d2d231`). In contrast, pure subsurface potential-field snapping even with spacing lock (`SNAPpot2px` `fc2548e7`, `Bands 3 & 18`) drops `SGMC` to `0.095178 (-0.00088)` because $60^\circ$ dipping normal faults place maximum horizontal gravity/magnetic gradients `200–600 m` down-dip in the hanging wall relative to the surface trace.
- **Decision Impact:** Enforced `min_sep = 2.8 px` (`min d_nn = 2.8284 px`), strictly provided `training_features.tif` bands (`Bands 3, 12, 18, 19`, zero un-supplied external LiDAR), and exact collar preservation (`2,171` `2–3 px` dots untouched; `100%` of moved dots at `d_cat > 3.0 px`) across `SNAPridge2px`, `SNAPpot2px`, and `SNAPscarp2px` (`scripts/build_snap_variants.py`, `tests/test_snap_axis.py`). Promoted `SNAPscarp2px` (`fae77ff4`) as the primary `SNAP` candidate (`Slot 2`) and `SNAPpot2px` (`fc2548e7`) as a negative-control diagnostic.
