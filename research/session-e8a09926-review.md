### 3.3 Mathematical Mechanism Under Distance-Weighted F-beta ($\text{DTI}$)

The competition metric (`vendor/gems57/metric.py`, verified against the [official problem description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/)) is pooled distance-weighted $F_\beta$ with $\alpha = 0.2$ (precision weight), $\beta = 0.8$ (recall weight), and a $300\text{ m}$ ($R = 3\text{ px}$ at $100\text{ m/px}$) linear triangular decay kernel $K(d) = \max(0, 1 - d/3)$:
$$\text{DTI} = \frac{P \cdot R}{0.2 P + 0.8 R}, \qquad P = \frac{\sum_{i \in \hat{Y}} w(i, Y_{\text{hidden}})}{|\hat{Y}|}, \qquad R = \frac{\sum_{j \in Y_{\text{hidden}}} w(j, \hat{Y})}{|Y_{\text{hidden}}|}.$$

1. **Kernel-Scale Declustering (`H19-5 -> D2.8`, `+0.0213` user-reported jump):** Because $R$ takes the **maximum** (not the sum) of kernel weights within $3\text{ px}$ of each hidden fault pixel, placing multiple dots closer than $\sqrt{8} \approx 2.83\text{ px}$ adds almost zero marginal recall $\Delta R \approx 0$ while linearly inflating the precision denominator $|\hat{Y}|$ (`121,131 -> 44,090`). Enforcing $d_{\min} = \sqrt{8}\text{ px}$ increased precision by $\sim 2.75\times$ without sacrificing coverage.
2. **2-Pixel Mapped-Catalogue Collar Subtraction (`D2.8 -> H33`, `+0.0040` user-reported jump):** Known mapped faults in `existing_faults.tif` are masked prior to scoring, and the hidden evaluation set $Y_{\text{hidden}}$ consists of **unmapped fault strands** (predominantly buried intrabasin steps, relay ramps, and range-front extensions) rather than parallel duplicates within $100\text{–}200\text{ m}$ of already-mapped traces. Consequently, the `6,436` dots in `D2.8` at $d_{\text{cat}} \le 2\text{ px}$ contributed $\Delta TP \approx 0$ against $Y_{\text{hidden}}$. Deleting them shrank $|\hat{Y}|$ from `44,090` to `37,654`, multiplying precision $P$ by $\frac{44,090}{37,654} = 1.17093$ (`+17.09%`) at fixed recall $R$, which maps through $\frac{\partial \text{DTI}}{\partial P} = \frac{0.8 R^2}{(0.2 P + 0.8 R)^2}$ to the observed $+0.0040$ gain (`0.2738 -> 0.2778`).

   - `data/bridge/sample_submission.tif` stores `float32` `NaN` on all `7,111,787` pixels (`57.92%` of the `3730 × 3292` grid) outside the scored footprint. Because IEEE-754 `NaN >= 0.0` and `NaN <= 1.0` evaluate to `False`, DrivenData's whole-array validator `np.all((arr >= 0.0) & (arr <= 1.0))` rejects any GeoTIFF containing `NaN` outside the footprint, `nodata` sentinel values (e.g. `-9999.0` or `-3.4028235e38`), or unclipped float32 overshoot (`1.0000001`).
2. **Automated two-decoder verification without human inspection (`scripts/audit_downloads.py` & `scripts/audit_submissions_dir.py`):**
   - `vendor/gems57/submission_writer.py` sanitizes predictions via `np.nan_to_num(..., nan=0.0, posinf=0.0, neginf=0.0)`, zeros pixels outside the footprint and on mapped `existing_faults.tif`, clips to `[0.0, 1.0]`, writes `nodata=None`, and runs `vendor/gems57/validate.py`.
