## 1. Why/how H33 (`h33-h33-2-b2…e5eb6e7e-zeros`, [USER] 0.2778) is the fleet best

Verified line-by-line in `research/h33-forensics-20261024.md` and re-measured this
session (`data/registry/h33_education.tif`, sha `c55bafc4…`):

1. **Pure subtraction chain.** H33 (37,654 dots) ⊂ SOLO-D2.8 ⊂ D2.8 ⊂ H19-5; no dot was
   ever added after H19-5. [REPO][DISK]
2. **Removed dots were ≤2 px from the catalogue.** DTI algebra
   (`vendor/gems57/metric.py`): removing a pure-FP dot always raises DTI
   (denominator −0.2). The two independent subtraction steps imply the same
   numerator T≈5.1–5.5k within 8% — exactly what pure-FP removal predicts. [INFERENCE]
3. **Kernel-spaced lattice.** NN spacing min 2.83 px, median 3.0 px = the 300 m
   triangular-kernel radius; adjacent dots waste FP budget because truth credit
   is a per-truth-pixel **max** while FP is a per-dot **sum**. [DISK]
4. **Non-hugging distance profile.** Median 19.6 px from catalogue, 41.7% of dots
   >25 px. Across 20 fleet rasters with [USER] scores, share>25 px has Spearman
   **+0.90** with score; catalogue-hugging trained classifiers (median 2–8 px)
   score 0.004–0.16. [REPO][DISK]
5. **Label-blind multi-modal ridge ancestry (H19-5)**, not a catalogue-fitted
   classifier. This session: GEMSDOE36 "Anderson PINN" ([USER] 0.2750) overlaps
   H33 by **0.974** — it is the same lineage, not independent confirmation. [DISK]

**Can 0.2778 be beaten?** Arithmetically yes: at 37,654 dots, +27–29% credit
reaches 0.3539, +59–63% reaches 0.4413 ([REPO] §3). No local instrument can
certify that gain in advance: hide-and-recover holdout scores H33 at **0.0067**
and BPWD's shipped TIF at **0.0001** on identical folds this session [DISK], so
holdout rank ≠ public rank for blind-fault seekers. Any claim of >0.2778 before
an organizer receipt would be a hallucination. What *can* be done is emit a
genuinely independent dot set with (a) corroboration (precision lever), (b) new
label-blind dots (recall lever), (c) stricter FP subtraction (collar lever),
while preserving H33's proven geometry. That is CON88.
