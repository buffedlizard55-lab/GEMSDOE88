## Why H33 performed well, and how strongly we can say so

The official DrivenData problem page says the target is geological faults indicative of geothermal resources; the metric is distance-weighted Tversky with a 300 m triangular kernel, α=0.2 and β=0.8. Those facts imply that redundant nearby dots can add false-positive cost while providing little incremental truth coverage.

Local SHA-pinned pixel forensics (`scripts/forensics_h33.py`, `evidence/h33_forensics_20261024.json`) measure a strict subset chain from H19-5 through D2.8 and SOLO-D2.8 to H33. The 6,436 pixels removed from D2.8 in the later stages are within two pixels of mapped faults; H33 spacing is concentrated near the metric's three-pixel support. These observations are consistent with H33's value being a combination of multi-modal lineament evidence and economical pruning of redundant/catalogue-adjacent predictions. They do **not** prove causation: the score `0.2778` is owner-reported, not copied from an organizer receipt, and the lineage runs are correlated. The official leaderboard was fetched on 2026-10-24 and showed a public best of `0.4413`; therefore the premise that H33 is today's competition high score is stale.

**Score labels:** H33 `0.2778` and the current leaderboard values are respectively USER-REPORTED and official public leaderboard observations, not HOLDOUT-DTI. The local run's `0.062936` is HOLDOUT-DTI (evaluator `gems57-pooled-hide-v2`, 22,893 withheld positives, 95% CI `[0.054201, 0.072122]`); it is not a public score and does not estimate a leaderboard rank.

### Why none was implemented here

1. The user's parallel-run instruction contains no explicit method paragraph in its text. The repo's frozen assignment names MAG-FABRIC-DISLOCATION and says no other candidate method in that lane; that method already has a run card, output and duplicate stop verdict.
2. Its final dots are 100% within three pixels of the positive-everywhere 17GEMSDOE registry witness. This is a protocol failure, not a signal to silently drop the witness or switch to a different overlap formula.
3. The cached training feature stack is not in this checkout. Public competition datasets are behind the DrivenData team login, and optical/SAR/USGS download endpoints are outside this sandbox's network allowlist.
4. No candidate outperformed the stored holdout incumbent in the completed lane; no slot was used. A new hypothesis list is not a substitute for a preregistered holdout result.
