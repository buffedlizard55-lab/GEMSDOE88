## Sources, viability, and licensing

- [USGS 3DEP](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program) says its elevation products are free and without use restrictions. This supports an officially obtainable terrain source for SPR; it does not prove every fine-resolution tile covers the exact study footprint or that the derived owner-mirror features were constructed correctly.
- [USGS GeoDAWN magnetic and radiometric release](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and) is an official release marked CC0, with coordinated lidar information. It supports viable potential-field/radiometric inputs for AWA, not a unique alteration or fault interpretation.
- [GDR INGENIOUS compilation](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391) is a publicly available Great Basin geoscience compilation with CC BY 4.0 attribution requirements; [the USGS blind-geothermal study](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/70271418) describes integrating gravity, magnetics and magnetotellurics. These support considering multimodal context for CBC, not its predictive performance.
- The exact cached raster hashes and bands are in `registry/hypothesis_queue.json`; `bash scripts/download_competition_data.sh` restored owner-mirror copies and `scripts/audit_research_state.py --check-inputs` verified them. They are **not** authenticated current organizer downloads. `u8` terrain and radiometric summaries are encoded indices, not proven metre-calibrated raw profiles. Independent source, coverage, band encoding, transformation and redistribution-license checks remain necessary before a build; suitable unquantized elevation profiles may require separately verified official tiles.
