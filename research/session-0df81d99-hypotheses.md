     $$Ra_f = \frac{\rho_0^2 g \alpha_T c_p k_f \Delta T H}{\mu \lambda_m} > \pi^2 \text{ (open top)} \quad \text{or} \quad 4\pi^2 \text{ (sealed caprock)}.$$
