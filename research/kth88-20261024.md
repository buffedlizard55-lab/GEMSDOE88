## Preregistered ranking, before implementation

[`registry/preregistration_kth88.json`](../registry/preregistration_kth88.json) records **four** hypotheses, the exact layers, transforms, non-fault mimics, catalogue rationale, novelty and cost. We selected only rank 1, **KTH-ANTISYMMETRIC-STEP**. It uses owner-derived K/Th (GeoDAWN bands 1–2) and supplied RTP (band 2). It estimates a 200 m smoothed, 1.8 km regional-detrended log K/Th field, takes the maximum *opposite-flank* difference at about 300 m over four directions, multiplies its rank by 0.25 + 0.75 × rank of a local RTP demagnetization trough, suppresses missing survey coverage and extracts directional crests. 37,654 binary dots have a >=3.5-pixel greedy lock and a 3-pixel mapped-fault collar. This differs from simple radiometric enrichment, radon-piedmont or generic magnetic-edge lanes. The named mimic is a potassic pluton margin or acquisition seam; radiometric K mobility is not specific to a fault.

**Obtained official/free inputs:** GeoDAWN data release is USGS CC0 (source link above). We used the hash-pinned mirrored derivative `geodawn_rad_u8.tif` (SHA-256 `c22420f75999030d7cc65c9e31e50d232ea6158423bca051613a18a8b20ba682`) because the official release's data host is not directly available within this sandbox. The derived K and Th cannot be authenticated independently to the official raw archive here; no hypothetical outside dataset was treated as already downloaded. **Post-experiment interpretation caveat:** the u8 raster exposes K/Th band names but *no physical-unit scale/offset tags*. A log ratio of their separately quantized integer indices (the exact code) is **not a physically calibrated elemental K/Th ratio**. The surface can be called a K-vs-Th *proxy* step only. This limits the hydrothermal mechanism and is another reason not to promote it; the preregistered method is not retroactively changed.

## Why H33 might have a higher *reported* score — and whether we can beat it

The exact D2.8 → H33 subtraction removes 6,436 catalogue-adjacent dots without adding any, increasing prospective per-dot credit under distance-weighted Tversky, while H33's kernel-spaced pattern covers potential off-catalogue corridors (disk evidence in [`h33-forensics-20261024.md`](h33-forensics-20261024.md)). Without hidden organizer labels, this mechanism does not prove the reported 0.2778 or predict its causal effect. **KTH-88 cannot be claimed to exceed H33 or the 0.4413 public leader.** Its hide-and-recover proxy is poor and fails the separate registry gate. Hidden labels may behave differently; neither this nor a projection is an organizer score.
