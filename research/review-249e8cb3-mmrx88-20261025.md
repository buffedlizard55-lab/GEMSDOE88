## 1. Why GEMSDOE32 "H33" is the fleet-best reported score (0.2778)

Every number in this section is **USER-REPORTED (no organizer receipt in this
repository)**. The geometry below was re-measured from the on-disk rasters in prior
sessions and re-verified in this one.

**H33 is optimal pruning of an ancestor lineament raster, not new geology.**
The lineage is a chain of exact subsets:
121,131 dots (0.1922) ⊃ 44,090 (0.2600) ⊃ 40,199 (0.2708) ⊃ 37,654 (0.2778).
The final step deletes 6,436 dots, **all of them ≤ 2 px from a mapped catalogue
fault**, leaving `share_le2 = 0` and a minimum dot spacing of 2.83 px.

**Metric economics explain the gain.** Under the DTI family used by this
competition (α = 0.2, β = 0.8; 300 m triangular credit kernel), a dot's marginal
truth credit `dT` falls (approximately linearly, in the ratio form
accept iff `dT > c·(1−k)`, `c = α·DTI/(1−α·DTI)`) while each emitted dot costs
budget through weighted false positives. The mapped catalogue's 300 m halo is the
most oversubscribed region of the map: additional dots ≤ 2–3 px from an existing
scratch re-cover truth mass that neighbours already credit, so `dT ≈ 0` while the
emission cost is full. H33's pruning removes exactly that redundancy, and its
remaining dots search where credit is still unclaimed.

**Fleet fingerprint analysis (this session; 16 on-disk lineage rasters with
user-reported scores; `.cache/fleet_fingerprint.json`).** Spearman correlation of
reported score with emission geometry: median catalogue distance **0.912**,
share of dots > 25 px from catalogue **0.873**, dot count **−0.166**, share ≤ 2 px
**−0.031**. Examples: H33 (median 19.65 px, share > 25 px 0.417, 0.2778);
an Anderson-PINN raster (19.24 px, 0.412, 0.2750); catalogue-hugging rasters
collapse (median 3.16 px → 0.0254; median 5.0 px → 0.0041).

**Caveats that bound this explanation.** (a) The sample is small and lineage-
confounded (nested subsets of shared ancestors; duplicated masks under different
names), so these correlations describe the fleet's history, they do not prove that
blind-search share *causes* score: blind share is a proxy for "searching beyond
the catalogue 300 m halo", which mechanically overlaps the region where most
*hidden* truth mass must live by construction of the task. (b) Session IR-88-120
records that the prompt's "leaderboard high 0.3195" is stale (public #1 was 0.4413
when fetched); neither figure is a GEMSDOE88 score. (c) IR-88-125 records that the
local evaluator scores the H33 raster itself at 0.0039 by construction — so no
HOLDOUT-DTI number is a leaderboard projection, ever.

## 3. Method (as implemented — deviations in §7)

`src/gems88/mmrx.py` (`mmrx88-16ch-v1`) builds 16 zero-catalogue channels on the
competition grid (EPSG:32611, 3730 × 3292, 5,167,373 footprint cells):

- **Geometry.** A structure tensor from Gaussian derivatives (σ = 3 px, integration
  σ = 8 px) of RTP + isostatic gravity + basement depth yields local strike **t**
  and cross-strike normal **n**, with **n** oriented toward increasing
  `depth_to_base_surf` (hanging-wall side).
- **Restoration operator.** High-passed RTP is resampled along strips offset ∓4 px
  across **n** and along-strike shifted `s ∈ {−4, −2, 0, 2, 4}` px; locally
  along-strike-smoothed correlations `corr_s` measure whether a displacement
  restores marker continuity. Gain = max_s corr_s − corr_0. The same operator on
  high-pass gravity gives a second-field gain; `min(gain)` requires *both* fields
  to restore. Strip-variance ratios encode hanging-wall short-wavelength
  attenuation; a step-without-gain channel encodes the unfaulted-contact mimic.
- **Named non-fault mimics (falsification channels):** unfaulted lithologic/intrusive
  contacts and depositional pinch-outs (step, no gain); GeoDAWN flight-line levelling
  seams (apparent offsets with no gravity restoration); reversely magnetized
  volcanic-flow margins (texture step with no depth-attenuation ordering).
- **Arms (3 experiments, budget exhausted):** Exp01 zero-catalogue PHYS-marginal +
  MMRX (HGB); Exp02 MMRX joint specialist fused with the proven base at β = 0.38;
  Exp03 — the preregistered submission slot — β = 0.58 / π_unmapped = 0.65 fuse.
- **Emission:** shared stratified Dinkelbach allocator (accept iff `dT > c(1−k)`),
  production budget 37,654 dots (the fleet's canonical H33-scale count:
  21,200 near + 16,454 far; near spacing 2.90 px, far spacing 3.12 px;
  collar ≥ 2.83 px).

**Verification before the holdout.** A synthetic elongated-marker field with a
known +3 px hanging-wall shift recovers on-line restoration gain 0.448 versus
0.083/0.072 off-line and 0.236 for a no-fault control (`tests/test_mmrx88.py`,
10 passed). Leakage canary over 123 features (100 zero-catalogue): max symmetric
AUC 0.769681 (`d2`, VIS fold-visible, seed 20); **MMRX max 0.539823**; zero-catalogue
max 0.58957 — all ≤ 0.90, no leakage flag
([`evidence/mmrx88_canaries.json`](../evidence/mmrx88_canaries.json)).

## 5. Failure analysis

**1. The 100 m grids have already destroyed the signal the operator needs.**
The marginal evidence predicted the pooled result before any model was fit:
catalogue-symmetric AUCs of the best MMRX channels are 0.540 (depth step), 0.533
(RTP step), 0.532 (mimic), and the restoration *gains* themselves — the core novel
signal — sit at 0.501–0.514, i.e. statistically indistinguishable from chance
marginally. Geophysical gridding interpolates 400 m-spaced flight lines onto a
100 m mesh with smoothing splines; the operator's restored-corrélation contrast
operates on wavelengths of a few pixels, exactly the band that gridding
attenuates and that shallow volcanic texture decorrelates over short distances.
The exp01 zero-catalogue arm (0.109017) edges the PHYS44 zero-catalogue control
(0.104163, stored record), so the channels are not literally empty — but the
residual information is roughly two orders of magnitude too weak to move a
107-channel incumbent whose VIS/PSGV blocks already span the recoverable
near-catalogue subspace.

**2. Uniqueness is lost in the allocator, not in the features.** To four
significant figures, MMRX reproduces PSGV's already-documented failure
(IR-88-127: overlap vs HGVC 0.7976; MMRX: 0.797578). Adding a zero-catalogue block
perturbs the posterior surface by small amounts; the Dinkelbach acceptance
threshold then reselects (almost) the same catalogue-anchored crest cells, and
the 3-px disk-overlap of two 37,654-dot fields drawn from near-identical optima
is ~0.80. Feature-level novelty does not imply emission-level novelty. IR-88-127
already stated that a future test "needs a different allocator/lane cap vs HGVC
pre-registered up front"; this session did not do that, and the consequence was
unavoidable in hindsight. Any future lane must engineer emission diversity
(hard exclusion/suppression radius against incumbent dot sets, or a joint
quota-constrained allocation) *as part of the preregistration*.

**3. The candidate's far-heavy fuse is a precision–coverage trade in the wrong
direction for this instrument.** β = 0.58 / π_unmapped = 0.65 shifts emission
mass afar: the candidate is (non-significantly) best in the blind > 25 px subset
(+0.003415 vs Exp02) but pays with FPW 73,866 vs 63,652 overall. Under a
hide-and-recover evaluator whose truth is still mostly ≤ 10 px from visible
segments (47% of withheld positives are in the far subset, but 53% are not),
sacrificing near-band precision loses more than blind coverage gains. Note the
circularity this exposes (IR-88-125): the same evaluator that punishes blind
search offline scores portal-best H33 at 0.0039. HOLDOUT-DTI and portal behaviour
disagree about where credit lives; neither is "the score", and conflating them
has misled several fleet lanes already.

## 7. Deviations and integrity notes (disclosed, not silently corrected)

- **Geometry drift vs frozen preregistration text.** The preregistration says RTP+gravity
  tensor at σ1.5/σ4 with a σ3 Gaussian window; the implementation uses an RTP+gravity+depth
  tensor at σ3/σ8 with along-strike smoothing, because the σ1.5 texture tensor
  failed syntactic validation on synthetic markers. The preregistration JSON was
  **not** rewritten; the discrepancy is recorded here and in the run card's
  mechanism string.
- **Candidate-slot wording.** The preregistration's prose calls Exp02 the candidate
  architecture with Exp03 as "alternative-fuse slot"; the runner designated Exp03
  `candidate` before folds ran. After seeing partial folds (Exp02 ahead in every
  one), this session **refused** to redesignate Exp02 as the candidate. Exp02 also
  loses to the BPWD bar pooled, so no designation change could have promoted
  anything; the point is that the decision was not available to make.
- **Fast-controls mode.** Recomputing the full BPWD suite in-run cost ≈13 min and
  ≈3.7 GB RSS for fold 1 alone (3.8 GiB sandbox) and would have blown the 2-hour
  budget. The runner therefore (a) reproduced BPWD seed-20 NW exactly —
  recomputed 0.191933 ≡ stored `evidence/bpwd88_holdout_20261025.json` — and
  (b) used stored BPWD fold scores/pooled bar for the remaining folds. Consequence:
  candidate-vs-BPWD is a point delta (−0.017613) with per-fold deltas, **not** a
  block-paired CI (`ci95: null`); `fast_controls_mode: true` is recorded in the
  evidence and run card. The paired CI that does exist (candidate − Exp02) is
  fully paired in-run and excludes zero.
- **Uniqueness policy version.** `binding_duplicate_decision_v4` in
  `vendor/gems57/uniqueness.py` implements exactly the versioned template fix
  requested by IR-88-128 (classify dot-fields vs continuous surfaces; compare
  continuous surfaces via like-for-like top-N), with the literal v3 decision
  nested and reported verbatim. It is a **proposal pending owner/policy sign-off**,
  not a self-granted exemption: this candidate fails v4 on dot-field overlap
  (0.798 vs HGVC), and fails the literal rule as well, so no verdict in this
  session depends on the interpretation.
- **Portal range error.** The reported "Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]"
  failure cannot fire on this package: independently reopened, the TIFF is one
  float32 band, values exactly {0, 1}, no NaN, nodata unset, zeros outside the
  footprint, CRS/shape/transform identical to the pinned sample submission; the
  ZIP contains exactly that one TIFF byte-for-byte. This is local format
  correctness, **not** organizer acceptance; without the originally uploaded
  bytes/receipt, the historical failure itself cannot be diagnosed further.
