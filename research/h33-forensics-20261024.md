## 1. The metric, algebraically (so the later arithmetic is checkable)

For a binary dot set with *n* dots, truth set G, and kernel k(d)=max(1-d/3px,0):
T = sum over truth pixels of the best kernel credit of any dot (a **max** per truth pixel);
M = sum over dots of the best kernel credit to any truth pixel; FP = n - M (a **sum** over dots).

    DTI = T / (0.8|G| + 0.2 (T + n - M))                                   (vendor/gems57/metric.py, tests vs brute force)

Consequences: (i) two adjacent dots on one fault add ~0 to T but add ~0.2 to the denominator, so dots are spaced about the
kernel radius; (ii) removing a dot with marginal truth credit dT and self-credit q lowers the denominator by 0.2·(dT + 1 - q) and the numerator by dT, so a removal
raises DTI iff dT < 0.2·DTI·(dT + 1 - q); for a pure false positive (dT = q = 0) it **always** helps (the denominator falls by exactly 0.2).
For a non-redundant dot (dT = q = k) the break-even is k = 0.2·DTI, i.e. **0.0556 at DTI 0.2778** (`vendor/gems57/metric.py`);
(iii) predictions on masked known faults cost nothing.

## 2. What H33 *is*, measured on the files (`scripts/forensics_h33.py` -> `evidence/h33_forensics_20261024.json`) [DISK]

* **Exact pixel-subset chain.**  H33 (37,654 dots) is a strict subset of SOLO-D2.8 (40,199), which is a strict subset of D2.8 (44,090),
  which is a strict subset of H19-5 (121,131).  **No step adds a dot** (`added_vs_a = 0` everywhere).
* **The only dots ever removed after D2.8 lie within 2 px of the catalogue**: 6,436 dots, d_cat in {1,2} px.  H33's smallest distance to any
  mapped fault is 2.236 px; 0 % of its dots are within 2 px, 5.8 % within 3 px, median 19.6 px, 35.8 % beyond 30 px.
* **Lattice geometry.**  Nearest-neighbour spacing: minimum 2.83 px, median 3.00 px, 81 % between 2 and 4 px: dots are placed about one kernel
  radius apart, which is what the max-vs-sum structure of the metric rewards.
* **No single band explains it.**  Largest single-band separation of dots vs footprint is detrended slope (AUC 0.657, dots avoid both the flattest
  and the steepest decile); all other bands lie within 0.5 +/- 0.08.  H33 is a multi-modal ridge product, not a one-band threshold.

## 3. What the reported lineage implies [OWNER + INFERENCE]

Reported public scores along the chain: H19-5 0.1922 -> D1.5 0.2477 (60,069 dots) -> D2.8 0.2600 -> SOLO-D2.8 0.2708 -> **H33 0.2778**.
Because every step is a pure subtraction (section 2), **each reported gain is attributable to the removed dots**, and by the algebra above
a removal can only help if those dots were below the marginal bar.

*Assume* the removed collar dots were pure false positives (credit 0).  Then T·(1/DTI0 - 1/DTI1) = 0.2·m (`lineage_arithmetic_INFERENCE`
in the JSON):

| step | m removed | implied T | credit per remaining dot | implied public positive pixels |G| |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| D2.8 -> SOLO-D2.8 | 3,891 | 5,073 | 0.126 | 12.5-13.4 k |
| SOLO-D2.8 -> H33 | 2,545 | 5,470 | 0.145 | 14.3-15.2 k |

The two independent steps give T within 8 % of each other, which is what the pure-FP assumption predicts [INFERENCE, not proof].
Reading: the *public* test set holds roughly **13-15 thousand fault pixels**; H33 captures about **36 %** of that credit (T/|G|), and an
average H33 dot is worth about **0.14**, i.e. only ~2.6x the 0.0556 break-even.  H33 is limited as much by precision (~10-15 % of dots hit)
as by recall.  The 0.2778 -> 0.3539 (first-round cut) gap needs T of about 7 k at the same dot count (+28 %); the gap to 0.4413 needs about +55 %.

## 4. The distance-to-catalogue fingerprint (`scripts/calibrate_proxies.py`, `scripts/fleet_distance_prior.py`) [DISK + OWNER]

Twenty rasters with owner-reported scores are on disk (H33 lineage, GEMSDOE28/33/36/49/63/66/70/73/75/76/80/81, 16/19GEMSDOE).
For each, the share of dots more than 25 px (2.5 km) from any catalogue pixel:

* Spearman rho with the reported score: **+0.90** (n=20), +0.85 (score >= 0.15, n=17), +0.61 (score >= 0.23, n=11).
  Leave-one-out linear fit: RMSE 0.036, LOO rho 0.85 (`evidence/fleet_distance_prior_20261024.json`).
* **Baseline matters.**  47.2 % of the scored footprint is more than 25 px from the catalogue (median footprint distance 23.1 px).  H33 sits at
  41.7 % (median 19.6 px): it is *not* far-biased, it simply does **not hug** the catalogue.  The low scorers are the huggers:
  GEMSDOE81 (median 2.2 px) reported 0.0041, GEMSDOE80 (median 8.0 px, 12 % beyond 25 px) 0.0943.
* It is a **necessary screen, not a selector**: GEMSDOE76 and 66 pass the screen yet report 0.1555 and 0.1152 (the linear fit over-predicts them by
  0.05-0.08).  Random dots with H33's distance profile would also pass it.
* The same table shows what the *other* proxies do: SGMC-based truth DTI is **anti**-correlated with the reported score in the top regime
  (rho -0.93, n=11, score >= 0.23) and catalogue hide-and-recover scores H33-class rasters at ~0 by construction (IR-88-20/35).  No instrument
  in this repository rank-orders the high public scores except this distance fingerprint.

## 5. Mechanism summary: why H33 is highest

1. **Subtraction of pure-FP dots near mapped faults** (sections 2-3): verified numerically; causal attribution inferred.
2. **A non-hugging distance profile** (section 4): verified; it is the best single predictor of the reported ordering.
3. **Kernel-spaced lattice** (about 2.83-3 px): verified geometry; matches the metric algebra.
4. **A label-blind multi-modal ridge surface** (H19-5): described on the 19GEMSDOE site as multi-line corroborated topographic/geophysical ridges;
   *not independently audited in this session* [UNKNOWN beyond that description].  Surfaces trained to fit the catalogue (this repository's
   ADHR, and my production attempt 1) concentrate dots at known faults, the profile the fleet shows to fail.

## 6. Can it be beaten?

* **Incrementally (likely small):** continuing the subtraction (e.g. a 3-px collar) is the only lever with controlled-experiment support in the
  lineage; the siblings that tested it (GEMSDOE89-92) carry no scores.  I cannot claim a number.
* **Structurally (what P(win) needs):** +28 % to +55 % credit at the same dot count.  This session's three pre-registered hypotheses did **not**
  deliver it on the holdout (`research/hypotheses-20261024.md`).  Independent surfaces in the fleet top out at 0.23-0.25 (GEMSDOE70, 49, 63).
* **Therefore:** P(a new unique raster beats 0.2778 on the public board) is low and **cannot be estimated from available instruments**; P(reaching the
  0.3539 first-round cut) is lower still.  Do not spend a slot on a raster whose only support is a hide-and-recover score (section 4).

## 7. Limitations of this analysis

All scores are owner-reported; the hidden test set, the public/private chunking and the per-fault weighting are undisclosed (threads 11527, 11550);
n=20 correlated rasters (shared ancestry) is weak evidence; section 3 assumes pure-FP removals; DrivenData data/submission pages require a login
that this environment does not have, so no receipt could be inspected and nothing was submitted.
