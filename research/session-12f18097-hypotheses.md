## Detailed Specifications of the Five Hypotheses

### 1. `H88-BFLX` — Broken-Plate Flexural Footwall Unloading & Hanging-Wall Moat Biharmonic Residual (Vening Meinesz Elastic Plate Filter)
- **Specific layers:** `training_features.tif` `B15 depth_to_base_surf`, `B13 iso_grav_anom`, `B18 iso_grav_anom_hg`, and `B12 det_elev`.
- **Physical signature & mathematical transform:** Normal slip across a major Basin-and-Range fault breaks or sharply bends the upper-crustal elastic plate (effective elastic thickness $T_e \approx 3\text{–}8\text{ km}$, flexural parameter $\alpha = [4D / (\Delta\rho g)]^{1/4} \approx 8\text{–}18\text{ km}$; Vening Meinesz, 1941; Buck, 1988). Unlike unfaulted sag basins whose basement depth obeys continuous-plate biharmonic flexure $(D \nabla^4 + \Delta\rho g) w = q$, a broken-plate master fault generates an **antisymmetric coupled dipole**: an exponentially decaying hanging-wall flexural moat in `B15 depth_to_base_surf` paired across a sharp inflection locus with footwall unloading uplift in `B12 det_elev` and `B13 iso_grav_anom`. In 2D Fourier space, compute the broken-plate biharmonic residual $R_{\mathrm{flex}}(\mathbf{x}; \alpha) = \mathcal{F}^{-1}\{ [1 + (\alpha |\mathbf{k}|)^4] \widehat{B15}(\mathbf{k}) \} - B15_{\mathrm{smooth}}$, paired with the cross-inflection antisymmetry product between hanging-wall moat curvature and footwall `B12`/`B13` uplift within `±6 km` normal to the `B18` strike.
- **Why it catches blind faults missing from USGS/INGENIOUS:** Rapidly aggrading piedmont fans and Quaternary lake beds bury surface fault scarps (`B19 det_elev_slope -> 0`), causing USGS QFaults to omit synthetic intrabasin master faults (`4–12 km` basinward of the range front), whereas the `8–18 km` crustal flexural moat-and-uplift couple persists intact in `B15` and `B13`.
- **Named non-fault mimic & rejection test:** Unfaulteddifferential compaction over symmetric paleovalleys or caldera ring moats (`gdr_volcanic_vents_in_footprint.csv`). Reject where the cross-strike profile is symmetric (no footwall unloading shoulder) or concentric around mapped volcanic centers.
- **How it differs from `src/gems88/`:** `qts88.py` decoupled pointwise `B15` thickness trends via local quantile regression, `bgcd88.py` measured local curvature divergence, and `tdkl88.py` used shallow `500–2500 m` potential-field continuation; **no module in `src/gems88/` applies an `8–18 km` broken-plate biharmonic flexural filter $(1 + \alpha^4 |\mathbf{k}|^4)$ or tests Vening Meinesz moat-uplift antisymmetry**.

### 2. `H88-PCBW` — Piedmont Coalescing-Bajada Contour-Inflection Waveguide (`5–12 km` Topographic Plan-Curvature Zero-Locus)
- **Specific layers:** `data/external/geodawn_dem_a2_100m.tif` (true undemanded elevation $z$), `data/external/topo_10m_derived_100m_u8.tif` (`B06 plan_curv_200m_q`, `B07 prof_curv_200m_q`), `data/external/hydro_3dep_100m_u8.tif` (`B01 log1p_area_q`, `B05 hand_q`), and `B15 depth_to_base_surf`.
- **Physical signature & mathematical transform:** Individual alluvial fans radiating onto an unfaulted basin floor have convex-outward semicircular elevation contours ($\kappa_{\mathrm{plan}} > 0$). Where a blind piedmont fault downdrops the distal fan apron, adjacent fans are forced to coalesce along a **rectilinear `5–12 km` strike-aligned contour straightening / plan-curvature inflection waveguide** ($\kappa_{\mathrm{plan}} \to 0$ coupled with a subtle down-fan profile-curvature step $\partial_n \kappa_{\mathrm{prof}}$) even when local slope `B19` is `< 1.5°` and no discrete scarp survives. Compute the multi-kilometer (`L = 6 km`) directional Radon/line-integral coherence of $|\nabla z|$-weighted topographic contour tangent alignment on `geodawn_dem_a2_100m.tif` where `100 m < B15 < 1200 m`, gated against incised active thalwegs (`hydro_3dep` `B01` high / `B05` zero).
- **Why it catches blind faults missing from USGS/INGENIOUS:** Aerial-photo and reconnaissance field mapping (`existing_faults.tif`) systematically miss sub-meter distributed piedmont fault zones reworked every century by sheetfloods on active bajadas, yet the multi-fan coalescing toe alignment integrates deformation over `10^4–10^5 yr` across `5–12 km` along strike.
- **Named non-fault mimic & rejection test:** Pleistocene pluvial shorelines (Lake Lahontan `~1330–1340 m` highstand benches) and axial river terrace risers. Reject any candidate contour waveguide that is approximately horizontal (`|∂z/∂s| ≈ 0` at constant elevation around a closed basin contour, unlike a faulted bajada whose elevation rises and falls across fan apices) or lacks subsurface `B15`/`B18` gradient support.
- **How it differs from `src/gems88/`:** `pfsr.py` used pointwise `1 m` LiDAR step height (`3dep_1m_step_metrics_100m.tif`), `drkn.py` tracked channel knickpoints, `fanapex.py` measured fan-apex offsets at range fronts, and `H88-SPR` models transverse scarp steps; **none tracks the `5–12 km` along-strike coalescing-bajada plan-curvature straightening waveguide on true elevation contours**.

### 3. `H88-STDD` — Salem Magnetic Tilt-Depth (`|∇B06|⁻¹` on `B06 = 0`) vs Gravity Basement-Depth (`B15`) Discordance
- **Specific layers:** `training_features.tif` `B06 tc` (magnetic tilt angle $\theta$), `B02 rtp`, `B03 tmi_hg`, `B15 depth_to_base_surf`, `B18 iso_grav_anom_hg`, plus `data/external/gdr_volcanic_vents_in_footprint.csv`.
- **Physical signature & mathematical transform:** By the Salem et al. (2007, *Leading Edge*) tilt-depth theorem for vertical magnetic contacts under reduced-to-pole (`B02`) transformation, the zero contour $\theta = \mathrm{B06} = 0$ lies directly over the contact edge and the reciprocal horizontal gradient of tilt angle estimates magnetic source top depth: $z_{\mathrm{mag}} = |\nabla \mathrm{B06}|^{-1}$ (calibrated against flight-height offset $h_0$ from GeoDAWN survey specifications). Compare $z_{\mathrm{mag}}$ along `B06 = 0` zero-crossing chains against gravity-derived basement depth $z_{\mathrm{grav}} = \mathrm{B15}$:
  - **Shallow volcanic flow / mafic dike mimic:** $z_{\mathrm{mag}} \ll \mathrm{B15}$ and `B18 iso_grav_anom_hg` weak (intra-fill basalt or shallow dike above deep unfaulted basin fill).
  - **True basement-cutting blind fault:** $z_{\mathrm{mag}} \approx \mathrm{B15}$ with co-aligned `B18` horizontal gravity gradient, **or** hydrothermal magnetite destruction where $z_{\mathrm{mag}} > \mathrm{B15}$ along a gravity-confirmed step.
- **Why it catches blind faults missing from USGS/INGENIOUS:** Neogene volcanic cover and intra-basin basalt flows obscure bedrock faults across northern Nevada (`USGS GeoDAWN` / Surprise Valley studies), causing pure magnetic-edge detectors (`B06`, `B03`) to flood volcanic plateaus with false positives while missing deep basement steps. Tilt-depth discordance isolates contacts rooted at the true Mesozoic/Tertiary basement interface (`B15`).
- **Named non-fault mimic & rejection test:** Unfaulted intra-sedimentary basalt flow edges, shallow Miocene dike swarms, and volcanic necks (`gdr_volcanic_vents_in_footprint.csv`). Explicitly suppressed whenever $z_{\mathrm{mag}} \ll \mathrm{B15}$ without `B18` basement density offset.
- **How it differs from `src/gems88/`:** `tdkl88.py` compared multi-altitude upward continuation of `B02` and `B13`, `bpwd88.py` computed Baranov pseudo-gravity worms, and `psgv.py` used pseudo-gravity vertical gradients; **no module in `src/gems88/` applies the Salem tilt-depth estimator $|\nabla \mathrm{B06}|^{-1}$ on `B06 = 0` contours or cross-checks $z_{\mathrm{mag}}$ against `B15 depth_to_base_surf`**.

### 4. `H88-ZBOF` — Ziagos–Blackwell Lateral Geothermal Outflow Plume Back-Projection up Hydraulic Gradient to `B17` Root
- **Specific layers:** `data/external/nv_heatflow_wells_idw_100m.tif`, `data/external/nv_springs_kernel_100m.tif`, `data/external/geodawn_dem_a2_100m.tif`, and `training_features.tif` `B17 cond_surf`, `B15 depth_to_base_surf`, `B18 iso_grav_anom_hg`.
- **Physical signature & mathematical transform:** In Basin-and-Range blind geothermal systems (Ziagos & Blackwell, 1986, *JGR*; Blackwell et al., 2007; e.g., Desert Peak, Bradys, McGinness Hills), buoyant fluids ascend a narrow blind fault conduit and then spread **laterally `1–6 km` down the shallow water-table gradient** within permeable piedmont aquifers, producing an asymmetric tear-drop shallow temperature/heat-flow plume whose peak is displaced **down-slope** from the actual fault! Placing dots at raw `nv_heatflow_wells_idw` maxima (`HVG-36` / pointwise thermal conjunctions) therefore misses the fault by `10–60 px` (`> 300 m` DTI kernel = `0` credit!). Instead, integrate `nv_heatflow_wells_idw` backward up the smoothed topographic/phreatic gradient $+\nabla z_{\mathrm{smooth}}$ (`geodawn_dem_a2_100m.tif`) using the Ziagos–Blackwell 1D aquifer Peclet decay kernel $\exp(-\Delta s / \lambda_{\mathrm{outflow}})$ ($\lambda_{\mathrm{outflow}} \in [1.5, 4.5]\text{ km}$) to locate the **up-gradient plume apex** where lateral thermal decay truncates sharply against a high-conductivity clay-cap / upflow root (`B17 cond_surf`) and basement step (`B18`). *(Explicit caveat per `session-2b5a8383-review.md`: smoothed surface topography is only a first-order proxy for phreatic head direction and must be masked across closed playa sinks.)*
- **Why it catches blind faults missing from USGS/INGENIOUS:** Blind geothermal upflow zones (`75%` of Great Basin systems lack surface fumaroles or mapped QFault scarps; Faulds & Hinz, 2015) are systematically offset up-gradient from shallow temperature-gradient wells and down-slope warm springs.
- **Named non-fault mimic & rejection test:** Conductive heat-flow highs over radiogenic granitoid plutons (`radiometrics_geodawn_100m_u8.tif`) or closed-basin evaporite brines (`B17` high on flat playas). Reject where down-gradient plume asymmetry is absent or where `hydro_3dep` `B05 hand_q ≈ 0` indicates an evaporite playa floor.
- **How it differs from `src/gems88/`:** `hgvc.py` linked thermal anomalies along upslope *strike/sinter* ridges, and `vcaz.py` evaluated pointwise vertical 2-m probe Peclet ratios; **no module in `src/gems88/` performs Ziagos–Blackwell lateral aquifer plume back-projection from down-gradient thermal tails to the up-gradient blind fault apex**.
