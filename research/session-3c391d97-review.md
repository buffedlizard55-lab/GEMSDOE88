## 3. Exact Diagnosis of `"Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]"` Portal Error

### 3.1 Four Root Causes in Historical `GEMSDOE` Submissions

When a GeoTIFF is uploaded to the [DrivenData submission portal](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/submissions/), the ingestion validator reads Band 1 and checks `np.isfinite(arr).all() and (arr >= 0.0).all() and (arr <= 1.0).all()` **before** masking out the `7,111,787` outside-footprint cells:

1. **Writing `NaN` outside the study footprint (`sample_submission.tif` trap):** `data/bridge/sample_submission.tif` stores `NaN` on all `7,111,787` outside-footprint pixels (`57.92%` of the `3292 x 3730` grid). Copying that `NaN` mask into a submission causes `np.isfinite` / `(arr >= 0.0) & (arr <= 1.0)` to evaluate to `False`, triggering `"Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]"`.
2. **Numeric `nodata` sentinels (`-9999.0`, `-3.4028235e+38`, `255`):** Setting `profile["nodata"] = -9999.0` writes literal `-9999.0` float32 values into masked pixels; when read via `ds.read(1)` (with `masked=False`), `-9999.0 < 0.0` fails the `[0, 1]` check.
3. **Unclipped floating-point overshoot (`< 0.0` or `> 1.0`):** Logistic/isotonic blends, bicubic/lanczos resampling, or FFT/Gaussian filtering in `float32` can emit `-1e-7` or `1.0000001`.
4. **Propagating Band 9 (`tmi_vg`) `-FLT_MAX` sentinel values:** Band 9 (`tmi_vg`) of `data/training_features.tif` contains `-3.4028234663852886e+38` on `5,801,497` pixels (`47.25%` of the grid, including `634,124` pixels inside the active footprint). Any pipeline that fails to clamp non-finite/sentinel values before feature normalization propagates `-inf`/`NaN` into predictions.
