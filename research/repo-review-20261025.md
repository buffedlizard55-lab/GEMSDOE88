## H33 0.2778: what can and cannot be concluded

The repo's H33 forensics dossier reports an exact pixel-subset lineage from denser earlier raster versions, with progressively sparser placement and catalogue-distance collar pruning. That offers a credible metric-level explanation: the DTI numerator uses distance-kernel credit, while unnecessary extra predicted pixels can add false-positive penalty without proportionate new true-positive credit. Spatially thinning a line-like candidate may improve the ratio if it preserves coverage; removing points near already catalogued faults may also help where the evaluation masks known faults.

The evidence does **not** prove H33 was the best scientific model, nor that the same editing rule generalizes. A better DTI on one hidden leaderboard can arise from chance alignment with its unreleased target distribution; the local hide-and-recover task withholds known catalogue components and cannot reveal all off-catalogue test faults. The repository says no organizer receipt for `0.2778` was saved. Treat it as **USER-REPORTED**, not `ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED`. A higher leaderboard score than `0.2778` is therefore **not demonstrated** by this review.
