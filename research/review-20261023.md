## H33: what explains the result, and what remains unproven

The inherited prompt's **0.2778 is USER-REPORTED, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED**. At the latest versioned `GEMSDOE32` main commit checked through GitHub API on this review date, [`docs/index.html`](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE32/blob/780a5a534269c79adfe6d1d7d5aadead161332aa/docs/index.html) says `UNSCORED`; [`registry/submission_build.json`](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE32/blob/780a5a534269c79adfe6d1d7d5aadead161332aa/registry/submission_build.json) contains null `submission_score`, `score`, and `organizer_score` fields. Its 0.2747 value is explicitly a model projection, not a result. A later organizer result could exist outside that versioned record; this repository has no submission-page receipt to establish it.

All removed points in both comparisons are within 2 pixels of the owner-mirrored known-fault raster; no retained H33 point is within 2 pixels. This reproduces a **thinning/pruning rule**, not independent evidence that the surviving dots are new faults. It is scientifically plausible that pruning points likely to be redundant with mapped faults lowers unmatched prediction mass. It can also lower credit for genuine new faults near known ones. Without hidden truths, the sign and magnitude of that trade-off cannot be inferred from the raster comparison alone.

The organizer's [published DTI definition](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/#mathematical-representation) penalizes unmatched prediction mass and missed target mass differently (α=0.2, β=0.8) and applies a 300 m triangular credit kernel. Thus a sparse, inherited high-performing pattern can outperform a less selective parent if the removed points contribute little truth credit. This is a mechanism, not proof that the thinning caused the reported score. The user-provided **0.2778** is not a valid comparison target for the local spatial holdout absent a receipt and identical evaluation protocol.

## Limitations and next actions

- The data page and submissions page require participant authentication. The training stack, sample grid, known-fault raster, and sibling submission rasters used here are hash-pinned owner mirrors; their hashes establish byte integrity, not organizer provenance. Do not ask for credentials in chat.
- No complete inventory of every public, private, deleted, or unlinked sibling registry raster is included. The saturated witness alone is sufficient for the current negative overlap decision, but it does not certify global uniqueness of the candidate.
- The registry overlap wording makes a positive-support full-footprint continuous raster a universal veto. Under that literal definition every nonempty candidate has 100% forward overlap. Do not hide this contradiction by excluding that raster; protocol/selector authorization is needed before any reinterpretation.
- The H33 score claim conflicts with the latest versioned owner manifest and has no organizer receipt in this repository. Obtain a verifiable submission-page receipt through authorized participant access before treating it as a confirmed score.
- The historic portal error “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]” cannot be causally diagnosed without the original rejected TIFF and portal response. The current TIF passes local range checks; that does not prove portal acceptance.
- Before the next experiment: resolve the lane and overlap protocol explicitly; obtain the organizer-authenticated sample/features if access is available; preserve the current evidence; define a new preregistered method; and require it to beat the same-fold holdout incumbent with a positive paired interval before considering a separate slot selector.
